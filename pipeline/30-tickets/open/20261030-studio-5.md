## Why
Transactional email services (Resend, Brevo) forbid cold outreach in their
terms (Resend acceptable-use policy, 2026-10-13: "unsolicited messages of
any kind, including cold outreach"). A real mailbox on the studio domain is
the standard route for low-volume, personalised B2B email. The studio caps
sending at 20 cold emails a day across all tests, includes an opt-out line
in every message and never sends twice after a "no", which keeps the
mailbox inside Zoho's reputation-based limits (Mail Lite: 250-500 external
emails a day; bulk sending is not permitted). The first two weeks run at
5-10 emails a day to warm the domain.
