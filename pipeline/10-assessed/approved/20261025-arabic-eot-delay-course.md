- **Id:** 20261025-arabic-eot-delay-course

- **Method playbook:** research/playbooks/udemy-niche-course.md

## What it is
A 4-6 hour Arabic video course on EOT claims: FIDIC 1999 vs 2017 notices and
time bars, Time Impact Analysis, windows analysis, concurrency, evidence
registers, with Gulf public-works amendments, taught with P6 and Excel screen
recordings. Published on Udemy, with Arabic YouTube teasers as the funnel.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Arabic-speaking planners, site and contracts engineers moving into claims.
Udemy list price USD 49-89, typical net to the instructor USD 15-30 per
organic sale (37% share) or 97% of the price on the instructor's own coupon
links. One purchase per learner.

## Why it works now
No Arabic EOT or delay-analysis course surfaced in the research pass; the
Arabic P6 courses that exist are basic or stale. GCC awards stay high (USD
204B in nine months of 2026) while delays and re-awards continue, so claims
skills are in demand in the owner's own market.

## What would kill it
- Udemy's organic share (37%) and constant discounting: revenue per sale is low unless learners come through the owner's own links
- A consultancy or university publishing a free Arabic course
- Course production time if the owner must record everything
