## What it is
A 6-8 hour Arabic Udemy course taking a junior engineer from zero to a
client-ready P6 baseline: WBS, calendars, logic, resources, DCMA-style QA, and
a monthly update cycle, with downloadable XER exercises. Free Arabic YouTube
lessons as the funnel, 97%-share coupon links in every video.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Arabic-speaking junior planners and site engineers in the GCC, Egypt and the
Levant. Udemy list price USD 44-79; instructor net USD 15-30 per organic sale
or 97% through the owner's links. One purchase per learner.

## Why it works now
P6 is the baseline tool on GCC public works and Arabic-language training is
thin and dated. Recording once creates an asset that also feeds the EOT
course, the newsletter and the services with warm leads.

## What would kill it
- Udemy's 37% organic share and discounting: revenue per sale is low without owner-driven traffic
- Free Arabic YouTube courses already exist (AUC-labelled playlist and others): the paid course must add the QA and update workflow they lack
- Recording time (one-off 30-40 h of owner time)
