# Idea Brief: Fixed-fee financial models for GCC startups and SMEs

- **Id:** 20261026-startup-financial-model-service

- **Method playbook:** research/playbooks/fixed-fee-financial-model-service.md
- **Date:** 2026-10-26

## What it is
A three-statement Excel model (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow,
driver dashboard, 3-5 year forecast) built to a fixed fee for founders
raising money or applying to Qatar's incubators and funding programmes.
Sold by direct outreach to incubated startups in Qatar and on Upwork.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Founders of pre-seed and seed startups and owners of SMEs applying for
financing. Upwork's own cost guidance: cash-flow projection USD 500-1,500;
three-statement startup model USD 1,500-3,500; fundraising and valuation
model USD 3,000-6,000; median hourly USD 35 (range 25-65). Offshore modelers
charge USD 40-80/h. Purchase frequency: one-off per raise, plus update work
before each funding round (every 6-12 months).

## Why it works now
Qatar's incubators push dozens of startups a year toward "investment
readiness", and every funding application (QDB, QSTP Tech Venture Fund,
angels) needs a model. MENA venture funding rebounded to USD 7.5B across 647
startups in 2025 (Fiducia snapshot, Q1 2026). Most founders cannot build a
linked three-statement model; the owner's Excel modelling skill and an
agent that builds and audits the workbook turn a 20-hour job into a
reviewed 4-hour one.

## What would kill it
- Template vendors at USD 109 (Financial Models Lab) take the price-sensitive end.
- Incubators provide the model as part of their programme.
- Reputation risk if a model is wrong; needs the owner's review every time.
- Seasonality: demand follows cohort and funding deadlines.
