## What it is
Fixed-price automation of a small business's spreadsheet work: merging monthly
files, cleaning and validating data, VBA or Apps Script macros, a KPI dashboard
with a one-page how-to. Delivered remotely from the client's own files. Sold by
direct outreach to small-business owners and operations managers in the GCC and
by Upwork and Fiverr listings. Not construction-specific (owner hold).

## Who pays, how much, how often
Owners and operations or finance staff at small firms (trading, clinics,
agencies, e-commerce) who run the business on spreadsheets. USD 290 per
automation or dashboard (price test USD 150-900); refresh retainer USD 60 a
month; repeat work as new reports are needed.

## Why it works now
Small firms still run on spreadsheets and pay USD 500-1,500 per project on
Upwork for someone to make them reliable; the floor on Fiverr is USD 5 for
generic work, so the offer must be outcome-priced ("your month-end in one
click") and local (GCC time zone, Arabic and English). The owner's Excel
modelling skill (a general skill, allowed under the hold) plus agent-built
macros make a USD 290 fixed price profitable.

## What would kill it
- Price pressure from USD 10/h sellers if the offer reads as generic Excel help
- Clients' messy data; bounded by a fixed scope and one revision
- Upwork and Fiverr account availability from Qatar (unverified)
