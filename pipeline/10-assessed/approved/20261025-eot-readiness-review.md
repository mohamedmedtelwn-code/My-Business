## What it is
A fixed-fee desk review for a contractor who is about to submit or has just
received an Extension of Time claim: programme audit (baseline logic, float,
updates), entitlement and time-bar check against the contract, evidence gap
list and a one-page recommendation. Delivered in five working days from
documents the client uploads. Sold through LinkedIn outreach and an Upwork
profile.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Contracts or project managers at small and mid contractors and subcontractors
in Qatar, UAE and KSA. USD 490 per review (price test USD 290-990). Repeat
purchases per project phase; upsell to full claim preparation (owner time).

## Why it works now
Freelance marketplaces are shedding low-value gigs while spend per buyer
rises; experts bill USD 175-400/h. A fixed-fee review priced between the two,
delivered with the owner's P6 and claims tooling plus agent drafting, undercuts
consultancies and out-values gig sellers. Live delay activity in Qatar.

## What would kill it
- The owner's available hours (this is service income; the agent drafts, the owner judges)
- Confidentiality: clients must share programmes; needs a simple NDA the owner signs
- Professional-liability expectations: the deliverable is a review, not legal advice

## Platform and payout check (from research/platform-rules.md and management/payments.md)
- Platform usable by a Qatar-resident seller: Upwork account availability from Qatar unverified; direct invoicing via PayPal (route 2) is verified
- Payout route: PayPal via QNB; Upwork pays via PayPal or Payoneer
- Published volume caps and disclosure duties that apply: Upwork service fee 0-15%; one account per person
