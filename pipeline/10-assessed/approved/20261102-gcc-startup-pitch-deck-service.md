## What it is
A written-and-designed 12-15 slide investor deck (editable PowerPoint and
PDF) plus a one-page teaser, in English or bilingual Arabic-English, for
early-stage founders raising from GCC angels, accelerators and funds.
USD 490 (QAR 1,785) design-only from the founder's content, USD 890
write-and-design with two revision rounds; one-pager USD 120. Sold by
studio-domain email to founders listed in public accelerator cohorts and
startup directories, and as a Gumroad package.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Founders of pre-seed and seed startups, typically before an accelerator
demo day or a fund application. Fiverr category guidance: pitch decks
typically USD 200-250, funding pitch presentations USD 350-400; top
sellers from USD 160-245 with 1,000+ reviews, consultants from USD 834-1,530.
Freelance designers USD 500-3,000 per deck (Strategy Presentation, 2026);
top Upwork pitch-deck freelancers show USD 200K-600K lifetime earnings at
USD 79-100/h (Waveup, citing Upwork). One deck per raise, a refresh per
round, a one-pager and data-room summary as add-ons.

## Why it works now
Demand is visible at platform level (7,759 Fiverr gigs, top sellers with
1,000+ reviews) and the deliverable is a document the agents can write
and lay out end to end with the studio's slide tooling. GCC accelerators
publish cohort lists with company names, so founders are reachable
through their company addresses, and the same prospects already receive
the financial-model offer (20261026-startup-financial-model-service):
deck plus model is the bundle agencies sell at USD 5,000+. Bilingual decks
for Saudi and Qatari funds are a gap the USD 15-45 Fiverr tier does not
fill.

## What would kill it
- AI deck generators (Gamma, Pitch, Canva) at USD 0-20/month cover the "make it look fine" job; the paid tier survives on narrative and numbers, not slides.
- Founders are time-poor and price-sensitive before a raise; many pay nothing until funded.
- Design quality judged subjectively; revision loops can eat the fixed fee.
- Overlap with the financial-model service: share the prospect list, never email the same founder twice in a month (rule 15).
