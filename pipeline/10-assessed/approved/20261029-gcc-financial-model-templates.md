## What it is
A three-statement startup financial-model template in Excel and Google
Sheets built for GCC founders: QAR, SAR and AED currency options, VAT at 5%
or 15%, end-of-service gratuity accrual, WPS payroll timing, incubator and
QDB application outputs, Arabic and English labels. Sold on Gumroad at
USD 49 (QAR 180) for the model and USD 99 for the model plus a cap table and
runway tool, with a free one-page runway calculator as the lead magnet. The
downsell of the fixed-fee model service (20261026-startup-financial-model-service).

## Who pays, how much, how often
Founders and SME owners preparing a funding application or investor deck.
Gumroad anchors: Standard Financial Model USD 149+, 53 ratings, at least
USD 7,897 revenue; Venture Capital Model USD 349, 19 ratings, at least
USD 6,631 (Marketsy Gumroad trends, 2026); generic models at USD 29-49 with
few sales; Eloquens models USD 104-336 with 200-2,000 views each. One-off
purchase; repeat buyers are accelerators and advisers buying team licences.

## Why it works now
Financial-model templates are one of the few Excel products on Gumroad with
visible five-figure revenue, and none of them handle GCC specifics (VAT
rates, gratuity, WPS, incubator forms). Qatar's incubators push 52 new
startups a cohort (QSTP, Fall 2026) and QDB programmes need a model. The
template is a cheap first step for founders who will not pay USD 900 for
the service, and the studio already builds the service's workbook, so the
template is a by-product. Excel modelling is an owner-edge skill.

## What would kill it
- Base rate: the median Gumroad creator earns USD 72 a month; without the email channel the template sits unseen.
- Free templates from incubators and banks.
- A wrong formula hurting a founder's raise; every release needs a check run.
- Gumroad Discover sales carry a 30% fee.
