## What it is
A fixed-price build of a progress and cost dashboard for a small contractor:
S-curves, earned value, manpower and look-ahead from their P6 export or Excel
trackers, delivered as an Excel workbook (or Power BI file) with a one-page
how-to. Sold on Upwork and Fiverr Pro style listings and by direct outreach.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Project managers and planning engineers at small contractors and
subcontractors. USD 350 per dashboard (price test USD 195-900); a monthly
refresh retainer at USD 90.

## Why it works now
Generic dashboard sellers are plentiful and cheap; construction-specific
dashboards that read P6 exports and speak the client's language (EV, float,
look-ahead) are rare at the small-contractor price point. The owner's Excel
modelling and P6 data skills, plus agent-built templates, make a USD 350
fixed price profitable.

## What would kill it
- Price pressure from USD 10/h sellers if the construction specialism is not visible in the offer
- Upwork account availability from Qatar (unverified)
- Clients' messy data (bounded by a fixed scope and one revision)
