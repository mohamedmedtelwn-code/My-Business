## What it is
Writing and formatting of tender responses, RFP replies and commercial
proposals for small suppliers bidding on GCC public and corporate tenders
in IT, supplies, catering, cleaning, training, events, medical supply and
consultancy. Bilingual Arabic-English, structured to the evaluation
criteria, with a compliance matrix and a method statement. USD 390
(QAR 1,420) for a single proposal up to 15 pages, USD 890 for a full
tender file (technical and commercial envelopes), USD 190 for a one-page
capability statement. Sold by studio-domain email to sales and bid
contacts at firms that appear in public award notices and supplier
registers. Construction-works tenders excluded until 2026-11-25 (owner
hold) and then re-assessed.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Owners, sales managers and bid coordinators at suppliers of 5-100 staff
that bid two to ten times a year. Labeeb (Dubai) publishes GCC packages at
AED 2,500 (USD 680) per proposal, AED 4,500 (USD 1,225) tender-ready and
AED 7,500 (USD 2,040) full suite; Upwork guidance prices tender freelancers
at USD 500-3,000 per project; UK junior freelancers GBP 500-1,500 per bid;
top Upwork proposal writers show USD 60K-200K lifetime earnings at
USD 12-25/h. One proposal per tender, repeated at each bid.

## Why it works now
GCC procurement portals (Etimad, Qatar's public tenders, Abu Dhabi's
Ariba) have moved small suppliers onto structured, criteria-scored
submissions where a compliance matrix and a clear method statement decide
the score, and the regional provider charges USD 680-2,040 per document.
The agents already run the tender-alert digest prospect list
(20261029-gcc-tender-alert-digest), so every digest subscriber is a
proposal-writing prospect with a live deadline, which gives the email a
concrete subject. Writing, structuring and bilingual drafting is the
agents' core work.

## What would kill it
- Confidential pricing and company data in every bid: written terms, deletion after submission, no reuse across competing bidders in the same tender.
- The studio never signs, submits or guarantees compliance; a buyer expecting a submission agent will not fit.
- Overlap with the tender digest (same prospects): share one list, one email per firm per month.
- Construction tenders are the largest GCC category and are excluded until 2026-11-25.

## Platform and payout check (from research/platform-rules.md and management/payments.md)
- Platform usable by a Qatar-resident seller: direct invoicing yes; Gumroad for the capability-statement package
- Payout route: Gumroad (QAR) or PayPal via QNB
- Published volume caps and disclosure duties that apply: email rule 15; NDA-style terms in every order; no claims of win rates
