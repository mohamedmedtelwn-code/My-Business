# Who to ask (20 direct asks)

Count a person only if all three hold:
1. They are looking for a job in Qatar, the UAE or Saudi Arabia now, or have
   said they will be within three months (new graduates, people on notice,
   people whose contract ends, newcomers to the Gulf).
2. They are a professional contact or a member of a group you belong to
   (ex-colleagues, LinkedIn connections, alumni, Telegram or WhatsApp
   job-seeker groups), not family or a close friend.
3. You can message them directly today.

Ask in job-seeker groups too, one post per group at most, with the same text.
Keep the list of names private; this folder holds replies only, with a first
name and target role at most.
