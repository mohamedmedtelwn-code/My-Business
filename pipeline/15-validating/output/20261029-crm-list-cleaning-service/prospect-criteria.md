# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a firm only if all four hold:
1. A company in Qatar, the UAE or KSA with roughly 10-200 staff that
   visibly emails customers: a newsletter sign-up on the site, a Mailchimp,
   HubSpot, Brevo or Klaviyo footer, a loyalty programme, or an online
   store. Not construction contractors or planning consultancies until
   2026-11-25 (owner hold).
2. No in-house data team visible (no "data" or "CRM" roles on the careers page).
3. A marketing, sales, operations or founder contact with a published
   business address, or a role address (marketing@, info@) on the site.
4. The firm's own site or a public directory gives the address; no scraped
   private lists, no personal addresses.

Good sources: Salla and Zid store sites that publish an email, Qatar and
UAE business directories, exhibitor lists, SaaS and clinic sites with
newsletter forms.

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (company, role,
address, source URL). Replies are saved in replies/ with a first name and
company type only.
