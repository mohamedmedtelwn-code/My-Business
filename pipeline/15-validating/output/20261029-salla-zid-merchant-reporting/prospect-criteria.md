# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a store only if all four hold:
1. It runs on Salla or Zid (platform footer or StoreLeads listing) and
   sells repeat-purchase goods: coffee, beauty, perfume, abayas and
   fashion, supplements, pet supplies, home goods.
2. It shows signs of volume: 50+ products, reviews, active Instagram or
   Snapchat link on the store page.
3. The store site publishes a business email address (about half of Salla
   stores and 41% of Zid stores do).
4. No agency or ERP integration is advertised on the site (those stores
   already have reporting).

Good sources: StoreLeads Salla and Zid reports, platform showcase pages,
category searches on the stores' own domains. No construction-related
stores (owner hold, not relevant here but applied studio-wide).

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (store, platform,
address, source URL). Replies are saved in replies/ with a first name and
store category only.
