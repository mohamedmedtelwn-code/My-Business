# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a firm only if all four hold:
1. A company in Qatar, the UAE or KSA with roughly 10-200 staff whose
   website earns leads (clinics, law and accounting firms, trading
   companies, schools, hotels, stores, agencies). Not construction
   contractors or planning consultancies until 2026-11-25 (owner hold).
2. The agent's pre-crawl shows at least three measurable faults
   (Lighthouse mobile performance under 50, broken links, missing
   hreflang on a bilingual site, missing titles or structured data,
   layout breaks in RTL).
3. A marketing, digital or founder contact with a published business
   address, or a role address (marketing@, info@) on the site.
4. The firm's own site or a public directory gives the address; no scraped
   private lists, no personal addresses.

Good sources: Qatar and UAE business directories, exhibitor lists, clinic
and school listings, Salla and Zid stores that publish an email, agency
client portfolios.

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (company, role,
address, source URL, the three faults). Replies are saved in replies/ with a
first name and company type only.
