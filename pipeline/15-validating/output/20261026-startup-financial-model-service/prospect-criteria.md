# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a person only if all four hold:
1. They are a founder, co-founder or owner of a company in Qatar (or the UAE
   or KSA) that is raising money, applying for QDB or QSTP funding, or in an
   incubator or accelerator cohort in the next six months. Not construction
   contractors, planning consultancies or claims firms until 2026-11-25
   (owner hold).
2. The company has no finance hire (under roughly 20 staff) and the founder
   owns the numbers.
3. They are reachable as a professional contact or through a public cohort
   listing, not family or a close friend.
4. A business email address for them or their role is published on the company site or a directory.

Good sources: QSTP Fall 2026 cohort (52 startups), QBIC and Digital
Incubation Center cohort pages, Qatar FinTech Hub, Wamda and MAGNiTT funding
announcements for Qatar, founders in Doha startup groups, SME owners you
already deal with who mentioned a loan or an investor.

Agents build the list from public business sources only (company websites, directories, cohort pages, exhibitor lists, funding announcements): company, role, business email address, source URL, saved in prospects.csv in this folder. No personal addresses, no scraped private data, no family or contacts of the owner. Replies are saved in replies/ with a first name and company type only.
