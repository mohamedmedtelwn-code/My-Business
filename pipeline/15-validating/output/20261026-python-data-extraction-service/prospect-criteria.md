1. They own or run operations, sales, purchasing or research at a firm with
   roughly 2-50 staff (e-commerce reseller, trading company, recruitment
   agency, real-estate brokerage, marketing agency, market-research
   freelancer). Not construction contractors or planning consultancies until
   2026-11-25 (owner hold).
2. They visibly track something on the web by hand: competitor prices,
   supplier stock, job boards, property listings, tenders, directories.
3. They are a professional contact (client, supplier, ex-colleague, LinkedIn
   connection, business group member), not family or a close friend.
4. You can reach them by WhatsApp, LinkedIn message or email today.

Good sources: LinkedIn connections in Qatar, UAE or KSA with "E-commerce",
"Procurement", "Recruitment", "Sales", "Broker", "Founder" in the title;
Qatar and UAE e-commerce and recruiter groups; sellers on local marketplaces
you have bought from.
