# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a founder only if all four hold:
1. The startup appears on a public GCC accelerator or incubator cohort
   page, a startup directory or a public funding announcement from the last
   12 months (Qatar, UAE, KSA, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait); pre-seed or seed
   stage. Not construction-technology startups until 2026-11-25 (owner hold).
2. The startup has a public site or listing with enough material to redo
   one slide (problem, product or traction).
3. A founder or company address published on the site or listing, or a
   role address (hello@, team@); never a personal mailbox found elsewhere.
4. Not already on the financial-model test's prospects.csv in the last 30
   days; the two tests share one list and never email the same founder in
   the same month.

Good sources: accelerator cohort pages, startup directories, public
demo-day line-ups, funding announcements in regional business press.

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (startup, role,
address, source URL, the slide redone). Replies are saved in replies/ with a
first name and sector only.
