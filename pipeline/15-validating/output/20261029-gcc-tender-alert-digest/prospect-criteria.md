# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a firm only if all four hold:
1. A supplier in Qatar (first) or the GCC that appears in a public contract
   award notice, a published supplier register, or a tender results page
   in a non-construction category: IT and software, medical supplies,
   office and industrial supplies, catering, cleaning and facilities,
   events, training, consultancy. Construction works, contractors and
   planning consultancies are excluded until 2026-11-25 (owner hold).
2. Roughly 5-200 staff; no dedicated bid team visible (no "tenders" or
   "bid manager" roles on the careers page).
3. A sales, business-development or general business email address is
   published on the company site or in the register.
4. The firm is not already a subscriber of a named incumbent (no
   TendersInfo or TenderGlobal badge on the site).

Good sources: Monaqasat award notices, Qatar Chamber member directory,
GCC Tenders Gate public pages, Etimad award lists, exhibitor lists.

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (company, sector,
address, source URL). Replies are saved in replies/ with a first name and
sector only.
