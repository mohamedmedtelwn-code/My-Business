# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a vendor only if all four hold:
1. A business-software vendor (accounting, HR and payroll, POS,
   e-commerce, CRM, booking, project tools) that sells into Qatar, the UAE
   or KSA and advertises Arabic support or an Arabic site. Not
   construction-planning or scheduling software until 2026-11-25 (owner hold).
2. The agents have tested the product on a public free trial against the
   Arabic-ready checklist and the verified listing is live before the email.
3. A marketing, partnerships or growth contact with a published business
   address, or a role address (marketing@, partners@, hello@).
4. The vendor's own site or a public directory gives the address; no
   scraped private lists, no personal addresses.

Good sources: the vendors' own sites, GCC fintech and SaaS directories,
exhibitor lists of regional technology events, app marketplaces (Salla,
Zid, Shopify) filtered for Arabic apps.

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (vendor, product,
category, role, address, source URL, listing URL). Replies are saved in
replies/ with a first name and product only.
