# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a person only if all four hold:
1. They are a founder or senior partner of a company based in Qatar, the UAE
   or KSA with roughly 5-200 staff (startups that announced funding in 2025
   or 2026, consultancies, agencies, clinics, training providers, SaaS). Not
   construction contractors, planning consultancies or claims firms until
   2026-11-25 (owner hold).
2. Their LinkedIn profile shows fewer than one post a month in the last six
   months, or posts only company reshares.
3. They are reachable as a professional contact (connection, group member,
   someone who has spoken publicly so a sample post can be written from their
   own words), not family or a close friend.
4. A business email address for them or their role is published on the company site or a directory.

Good sources: Wamda and MAGNiTT funding announcements for Qatar and UAE
startups; QSTP, QBIC and Qatar FinTech Hub cohort pages; speakers at Doha
business events; partners of small Doha consultancies on LinkedIn.

Send the agents the public profile links by 2026-11-08 so the sample posts
are ready. List the names privately (not in this folder). This folder holds
replies only, without personal details beyond a first name and company type.
