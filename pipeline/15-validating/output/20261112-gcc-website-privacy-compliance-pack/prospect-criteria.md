# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a firm only if all four hold:
1. An online store, clinic, agency, school, real-estate office or service
   firm in Saudi Arabia or the UAE with 5-50 staff and a website that has
   a contact form, a booking tool, a checkout or analytics. Not
   construction contractors or planning consultancies until 2026-11-25
   (owner hold). Health and finance firms count only for the notice and
   are told in the email that a lawyer should review.
2. The agent's headless scan of the public site shows at least one fault:
   advertising or analytics tags (Google, Meta, TikTok, chat widgets)
   loading before any consent, no consent banner, no privacy-notice link,
   or an English-only notice on an Arabic-facing site.
3. An owner, marketing or operations contact with a published business
   address on the firm's own site, or a role address (info@, marketing@,
   hello@) on the site.
4. The address comes from the firm's own site or a public directory; no
   scraped private lists, no personal addresses.

Good sources: Saudi and UAE business directories, Salla and Zid store
directories, the website-audit, Google-listing and email-authentication
prospect lists (shared audience; reuse their domains and scan once for
all four tests).

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (firm, role, address,
source URL, tags found, notice present). Replies are saved in replies/
with a first name and firm type only. Only public page loads are made;
no form is submitted and no account is created on the prospect's site.
