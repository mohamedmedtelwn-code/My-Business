# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 direct asks)

Count a person only if all four hold:
1. A founder or co-founder of a startup in Qatar, the UAE or KSA at
   pre-seed or seed stage, listed on a public cohort page (QSTP, QBIC,
   Digital Incubation Center, Qatar FinTech Hub, Flat6Labs, Hub71) or in a
   funding announcement in the last 12 months.
2. The company has no finance hire visible on its site or careers page.
3. A company email address for the founder or a founders@ or hello@ role
   address is published on the company site.
4. Not already emailed for the financial-model service
   (20261026-startup-financial-model-service) unless they declined it.
   Not construction-related (owner hold).

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (company, role,
address, source URL). Replies are saved in replies/ with a first name and
sector only.
