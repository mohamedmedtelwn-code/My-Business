# Who to contact: Arabic Primavera P6 fundamentals and baseline QA course (Udemy + YouTube)

Target: Arabic-speaking junior planners and site engineers.

Pick people you can plausibly message (your own network first, then second-degree LinkedIn contacts, then public profiles in the GCC with a planning, project controls, contracts or claims title). Prefer people at firms with a recent award, re-award or visible delay (GCC Tender News, MEED, Zawya, Ashghal announcements). Never message more than 20 people a day. Do not use any list bought or scraped; every name is one you choose and type yourself.

Record each person's first name and company only in your own notes; this folder holds counts and replies, not contact lists.
