# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a firm only if all four hold:
1. A company in Qatar, the UAE or KSA with roughly 5-100 staff that
   visibly runs on at least two SaaS tools (a web form, a Salla or Zid
   store, HubSpot or Zoho, Google Workspace, QuickBooks or Xero badges, a
   booking widget). Not construction contractors or planning consultancies
   until 2026-11-25 (owner hold).
2. The agent can name one manual gap from the public stack (form without
   auto-reply, store without automated invoicing, applications by email,
   manual order confirmations).
3. An operations, admin, sales or founder contact with a published
   business address, or a role address (info@, operations@, sales@).
4. The firm's own site or a public directory gives the address; no scraped
   private lists, no personal addresses.

Good sources: Salla and Zid stores that publish an email, Qatar and UAE
business directories, clinic and training-centre sites with booking
widgets, agencies and consultancies with web forms.

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (company, role,
address, source URL, the named gap). Replies are saved in replies/ with a
first name and company type only.
