# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a business only if all four hold:
1. A clinic, dental practice, restaurant, cafe, salon, gym, car service
   or similar location-based business in Qatar, the UAE or KSA with 1-5
   locations and a Google Business Profile with at least 20 reviews.
   Not construction contractors or planning consultancies until
   2026-11-25 (owner hold).
2. The agent's audit shows at least three faults (unanswered reviews in
   the last 20, no Arabic or no English description, empty services,
   missing hours or booking link, no posts in 90 days).
3. An owner, manager or marketing contact with a published business
   address on the business's own site, or a role address (info@,
   marketing@, hello@) on the site or listing.
4. The address comes from the business's own site or listing or a public
   directory; no scraped private lists, no personal addresses.

Good sources: Google Maps listings by category and city, Qatar and UAE
business directories, clinic and restaurant listings, the website-audit
prospect list (shared audience).

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (business, role,
address, source URL, the three faults). Replies are saved in replies/ with
a first name and business type only.
