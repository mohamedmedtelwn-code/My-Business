# Prospect list rules (agent-built, 20 first, 30 more if needed)

Count a firm only if all four hold:
1. A company in Qatar, the UAE or KSA with roughly 10-200 staff in trading,
   distribution, logistics, clinics, schools, hospitality or facilities
   services, where paper records (supplier invoices, delivery notes,
   ledgers, forms) are part of the work. Not construction contractors or
   planning consultancies until 2026-11-25 (owner hold).
2. No visible document-management or data-entry team (careers page, site).
3. A finance, accounts, admin or procurement contact with a published
   business address, or a role address (accounts@, admin@, info@).
4. The firm's own site or a public directory gives the address; no scraped
   private lists, no personal addresses.

Good sources: Qatar and UAE business directories, chamber of commerce
member lists, supplier registers in non-construction categories, clinic and
school listings.

Agents save the list as prospects.csv in this folder (company, role,
address, source URL). Replies and sample files are saved in replies/ with a
first name and company type only; sample pages are deleted after the test.
