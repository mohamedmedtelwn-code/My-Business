# Idea Brief: GCC project-controls weekly newsletter (free, sponsor-funded, then paid tier)

- **Id:** 20261025-gcc-project-controls-newsletter
- **Category:** 1 Content platforms (newsletter), owner-edge adjacent
- **Method playbook:** research/playbooks/beehiiv-b2b-niche-newsletter.md
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## What it is
A weekly English email (Arabic summary at the top) for planners, project
controls and claims engineers in the GCC: contract awards and re-awards, new
delay and claims decisions, P6 tips, one tool or template of the week. Free on
beehiiv; sponsors (P6 training providers, schedule-tool vendors, recruiters)
from about 1,000 subscribers; a paid tier with the studio's templates later.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Sponsors: P6 course sellers, scheduling-tool vendors, GCC construction
recruiters. Paved's rule of thumb: USD 2.5-5 per 100 subscribers per placement
(USD 25-50 per placement at 1,000 subscribers; USD 250-500 at 10,000). Paid
tier later at USD 5-9 a month to about 0.6% of readers.

## Why it works now
GCC project news exists (MEED, Zawya, GCC Tender News) but nothing is curated
for planners and claims engineers, and nothing is bilingual. The studio
already produces the content (tender news, re-awards, P6 tips) while
researching its other businesses, so the marginal cost is near zero. The
newsletter also becomes the owned channel the rubric asks every other edge
business for.

## What would kill it
- Slow growth: sponsor revenue needs about 1,000 engaged subscribers; realistic month-6 revenue is tens of dollars
- beehiiv's free plan lacks direct sponsorships (Pro is USD 95/month at 1,000 subscribers: only when sponsors cover it)
- Copyright: summaries and links only, never republished articles

## Platform and payout check (from research/platform-rules.md and management/payments.md)
- Platform usable by a Qatar-resident seller: beehiiv publishing yes; beehiiv payouts and Stripe-based paid tiers from Qatar UNVERIFIED, so sponsors are invoiced directly and paid via PayPal (QNB) or bank transfer
- Payout route: PayPal via QNB for sponsors; paid tier only once a Qatar-compatible route is confirmed
- Published volume caps and disclosure duties that apply: sponsored placements labelled; CAN-SPAM style unsubscribe
