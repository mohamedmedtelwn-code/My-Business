## What it is
Translation and localisation of business content (websites, proposals,
product sheets, presentations, app strings, marketing posts) between Arabic
and English, priced per word, delivered by an agent draft plus the owner's
native review. Sold by direct outreach to firms entering the GCC and to
Qatar SMEs, and on Upwork/ProZ.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Marketing and operations teams of SMEs, agencies and SaaS firms. Aggregated
2026 rates: English-Arabic general USD 0.13-0.30 per word, specialised
USD 0.22-0.50 (FreelanceDesk, 12 sources); Arabic-English USD 0.14-0.22
general, USD 0.22-0.32 technical, USD 280-440 for a 2,000-word document
(FreelanceRates). Gulf-focused platforms quote USD 0.05-0.15 general.
Top Upwork profiles charge USD 15-20/h and show 248-310 jobs and USD 90K-100K+
earned. Frequency: recurring for marketing content (monthly), one-off for
documents.

## Why it works now
Arabic is one of the best-paid language pairs because qualified bilingual
supply is thin, and GCC-bound firms need Arabic content for Vision-2030 and
Qatar market entry. Machine translation produces drafts cheaply, so buyers
now pay for review by a native who knows Gulf business register; an agent
draft plus the owner's review is exactly that offer. The owner is bilingual
and writes both registers.

## What would kill it
- Buyers accepting raw machine output; per-word rates fall toward USD 0.03 (already seen on low-cost profiles).
- Certified or legal documents are reserved to Ministry of Justice-approved translation offices in Qatar; those jobs must be declined.
- Fiverr/Upwork rules on bulk AI delivery; disclosure of AI assistance where a client asks.
- Volume dependence: income scales with the owner's review hours.

## Platform and payout check (from research/platform-rules.md and management/payments.md)
- Platform usable by a Qatar-resident seller: direct outreach yes; Upwork and Fiverr UNVERIFIED; ProZ membership open worldwide (USD 180/yr Pro, optional)
- Payout route: PayPal via QNB; bank transfer in QAR for Qatar clients
- Published volume caps and disclosure duties that apply: Fiverr bars bulk AI output delivered to several clients; one account per person; no certification claims

## Time
- Time to first dollar (typical, from evidence): 1-3 weeks by direct outreach; 4-8 weeks on Upwork/ProZ for a new profile
- Monthly range at 6 months (typical small operator, from evidence; name the median if known): USD 300 - 1,500 at 2,000-10,000 words a month; median unknown
- Owner time per week after launch: 4-6 h review

## Distribution
Channel 1: direct outreach to 20 firms with English-only websites that sell
in Qatar or the UAE (SaaS, clinics, training providers, e-commerce), offering
a free 300-word sample of their homepage in Arabic and a per-word quote.
First ten customers: Qatar SMEs in the owner's non-construction network and
exhibitors listed on upcoming Doha trade-show sites. Second channel: Upwork
and ProZ profiles answering Arabic-English posts, plus a Fiverr
outcome-priced gig ("your landing page in Gulf Arabic, 48 h").
