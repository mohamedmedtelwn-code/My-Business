# Idea Brief: Pay-per-lead Meta campaigns for Qatar home-service SMEs

- **Id:** 20261026-qatar-home-services-lead-gen
- **Category:** 8 Affiliate and lead-gen (lead-gen into a service)
- **Method playbook:** research/playbooks/pay-per-lead-meta-campaigns.md
- **Date:** 2026-10-26

## What it is
Bilingual Facebook and Instagram lead campaigns (WhatsApp click-to-message
and lead forms) run for one home-service business per niche in Doha
(cleaning, AC maintenance, moving, pest control, tutoring), paid per
qualified lead, with the client funding the ad spend in its own ad account.
The studio sells the campaign set-up and lead qualification, never buys
media itself.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Owners of small service companies in Qatar that rely on word of mouth.
Reported lead values for local services abroad: USD 50-300 per lead
(SideGigLab 2026, US); a Meta case study shows 569 message leads for USD 158
of ad spend (USD 0.28 per conversation) for a local service business; GCC
agency benchmarks quote professional-services inquiries at AED 100-350 and
Qatar Google Ads home-services CPA modelled at QAR 206. Pricing to copy:
USD 10-25 per qualified WhatsApp lead or a flat USD 150-300 a month per
client, billed monthly.

## Why it works now
Qatar's small service firms sell by WhatsApp and Instagram, Meta CPMs in
Qatar are below the global median, and "answer in two minutes" wins the
job (Qatar lead-gen guide, 2026). Pay-per-lead with client-funded media
means the studio starts at USD 0 and the client pays for proven enquiries,
which is the offer agencies in the GCC already sell by retainer. An agent
builds the bilingual creatives, forms and the lead-qualification sheet.

## What would kill it
- Lead quality disputes; needs a written definition of a qualified lead and a shared sheet.
- Meta account restrictions on new ad accounts; the client's own account must be in good standing.
- Clients learning to run the ads themselves after month 2.
- Qatar requires the service business to be licensed; the studio must only serve registered firms.
- SEO-only variants (rank-and-rent) take 3-9 months and rely on Google organic alone (rule 9), so they are excluded.

## Platform and payout check (from research/platform-rules.md and management/payments.md)
- Platform usable by a Qatar-resident seller: Meta ads run in the client's account (client accepts Meta terms); service invoiced directly: yes
- Payout route: bank transfer in QAR or PayPal via QNB; needs the legal basis in ticket 20261025-studio-1
- Published volume caps and disclosure duties that apply: Meta advertising policies (no misleading claims, no restricted categories); WhatsApp Business messaging rules; no purchased engagement

## Time
- Time to first dollar (typical, from evidence): 3-5 weeks (sign one client, run 2 weeks, invoice the first leads); the Meta case study produced leads within days of launch
- Monthly range at 6 months (typical small operator, from evidence; name the median if known): USD 300 - 1,500 (2-5 clients at USD 150-300); median unknown
- Owner time per week after launch: 2-3 h (client calls, approving creatives, invoicing)

## Distribution
Channel 1: direct WhatsApp and visit-style outreach to 20 licensed home-
service firms in Doha found through Instagram and Google Maps listings with
under 500 followers, offering a two-week pilot paid only per qualified lead.
First ten customers: those firms plus service providers the owner already
buys from. Second channel: referrals from the first clients and a short
case-study post in Qatar business groups once a pilot has numbers.

## Legal / platform notes
The studio does not hold ad spend or client money; the client's Meta account
and card are used. Lead data is the client's; store nothing beyond the shared
sheet (Qatar PDPPL). Serve licensed businesses only. Income legal basis per
ticket 20261025-studio-1.
