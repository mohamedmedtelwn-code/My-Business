- **Id:** 20261025-arabic-excel-power-query-course

## What it is
A 6-8 hour Arabic Udemy course on modern Excel for office professionals:
Power Query clean-ups, Power Pivot and DAX basics, dashboard building, with
downloadable exercise files from finance, HR and sales data (no construction
content: owner hold). Free Arabic YouTube lessons as the funnel with
97%-share coupon links.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Arabic-speaking accountants, analysts, HR and operations staff in the GCC and
Egypt. Udemy list USD 44-79; instructor net USD 15-30 per organic sale or 97%
via the owner's links. One purchase per learner.

## Why it works now
Excel training is the largest skills category on Udemy, Arabic data courses
are few and recent, and GCC employers ask for Power Query and dashboard
skills in finance and operations roles. The owner's Excel modelling skill is
a general edge item allowed under the hold.

## What would kill it
- Udemy's 37% organic share and discounting (the median course has 217 enrolments)
- Free Arabic Excel content on YouTube; the course must sell the workflow, not the clicks
- Owner recording time (one-off 25-35 h)
