## What it is
A fixed-price package for professionals applying to jobs in Qatar, the UAE
and Saudi Arabia: an ATS-friendly English CV, an Arabic version, a LinkedIn
profile rewrite and a cover letter, delivered in three working days from a
questionnaire. Agent drafts, owner reviews. Sold by direct asks in the
owner's network and job-seeker groups, then Fiverr and Upwork listings.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Mid-career professionals and new arrivals in the GCC who need a CV that
passes applicant-tracking systems and reads well to Arabic-speaking
employers. USD 79 for CV plus LinkedIn (price test USD 49-149); Arabic
version USD 40 extra. One purchase per job search; referrals.

## Why it works now
GCC hiring runs in two languages and through ATS filters; local services
charge QAR 249 just for translation. The owner's Arabic and English and GCC
market knowledge (general edge items, allowed under the hold) plus agent
drafting deliver the bundle at a price under the local competitors and above
the Fiverr floor.

## What would kill it
- AI resume tools making the English CV a commodity (the Arabic version and local market framing are the defence)
- Reputation risk if quality slips; mitigated by the owner reviewing every CV
- Fiverr and Upwork account availability from Qatar (unverified); direct sales do not depend on them

## Platform and payout check (from research/platform-rules.md and management/payments.md)
- Platform usable by a Qatar-resident seller: direct invoicing via PayPal (QNB) verified; Gumroad can sell the package as a digital service order
- Payout route: PayPal via QNB or Gumroad
- Published volume caps and disclosure duties that apply: none; no claims of guaranteed interviews

## Distribution
Channel 1: 20 direct asks to job seekers in the owner's network and in GCC
job-seeker groups (LinkedIn, Telegram, WhatsApp communities the owner belongs
to). Channel 2: Fiverr and Upwork listings; Channel 3 later: a free "GCC CV
checklist" PDF on Gumroad as lead magnet.
