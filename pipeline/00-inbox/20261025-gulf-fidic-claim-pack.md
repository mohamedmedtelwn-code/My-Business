## What it is
A Gumroad download (Word and Excel, Arabic and English) with FIDIC 1999 and
2017 notice letters, a Sub-Clause 20.1 / 20.2 time-bar tracker, a claim
register and one worked Extension of Time example with the amendments common in
Gulf public-works contracts. Sold to planning and contracts engineers at small
and mid GCC contractors, reached by personalised LinkedIn messages.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Planning or contracts engineer at a contractor without a claims department.
USD 49 one-off (price test USD 29-79). One purchase per firm; later upsell to a
claim readiness review (separate brief).

## Why it works now
Delayed and re-awarded projects generate notices, time-bar deadlines and
claims. The research pass found no Arabic or Gulf-specific EOT template pack;
the English packs on sale are generic and priced at USD 0-15. The premium has
to come from Gulf detail, bilingual text and a worked example.

## What would kill it
- A consultancy releasing a free bilingual pack as a lead magnet
- Buyers wanting legal sign-off the studio cannot give (mitigated by a clear "not legal advice" disclaimer and owner review)
- Gumroad changing its payout rules for Qatar
