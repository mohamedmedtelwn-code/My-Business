## What it is
A monthly retainer that turns a founder's or executive's notes and a
30-minute call into 4-8 LinkedIn posts a month in English, Arabic or both,
with a profile rewrite in month one. Sold by direct outreach to founders of
funded GCC startups and to partners of small professional firms.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Founders, consultants and executives who want inbound leads. 2026 market:
entry freelancers USD 500-1,500 a month for 4-8 posts (Mylance, Foundera);
mid-tier USD 1,500-3,500 (Foundera survey; Windmill, 185+ clients);
per-post USD 50-150 entry, 150-300 mid. One Fiverr test gig for C-suite
LinkedIn ghostwriting: 47 orders and USD 4,200 net in 90 days (TecDigi).
Frequency: monthly retainer, typical tenure 3-6 months.

## Why it works now
Founders raising in the GCC (647 funded MENA startups in 2025) and
consultants selling to Vision-2030 projects use LinkedIn as their main
inbound channel, and most cannot write consistently in two languages. The
owner writes Arabic and English; an agent drafts from the call transcript
and the owner edits for voice. Entry pricing is low enough for a first
"yes" and retainers compound.

## What would kill it
- AI-generated posts flooding feeds; LinkedIn reach drops for generic content, clients churn.
- Clients expecting leads, not posts; needs a strategy layer to keep retainers past month 3.
- Owner time: every post needs voice editing; capacity is 4-6 clients.
- Reputational risk if a client's claims are false; decline exaggerated content.

## Platform and payout check (from research/platform-rules.md and management/payments.md)
- Platform usable by a Qatar-resident seller: direct outreach yes; Fiverr/Upwork UNVERIFIED; LinkedIn has no ghostwriting ban (client posts under their own account)
- Payout route: PayPal via QNB; bank transfer in QAR for Qatar clients
- Published volume caps and disclosure duties that apply: client, not the agent, publishes; no fake engagement or pods; Fiverr bars bulk identical AI output

## Distribution
Channel 1: direct LinkedIn messages to 20 founders of recently funded GCC
startups (Wamda/MAGNiTT deal lists) and partners of small Doha consultancies
whose profiles post rarely, with one sample post written from their own
public interview. First ten customers: those lists plus the owner's
non-construction professional contacts. Second channel: a Fiverr
outcome-priced gig ("4 bilingual LinkedIn posts from one 30-minute call")
and the owner's own LinkedIn posts showing before/after rewrites.
