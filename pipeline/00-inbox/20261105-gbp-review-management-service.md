## What it is
A one-time bilingual optimisation of a business's Google Business Profile
(categories, services, Arabic and English descriptions, photos shot list,
Q&A seeding, hours, booking link) at USD 190 (QAR 690) per location, and a
monthly retainer at USD 120 per location: every review answered in the
customer's language within 24 hours in the owner's approved tone, four
posts a month, monitoring for unwanted edits, and a one-page monthly
report. Sold by studio-domain email to owners and managers of clinics,
dental practices, restaurants, salons, gyms and car services in Qatar and
the GCC, with a free 10-point profile audit as the hook.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Owners and marketing staff of location-based businesses with 1-5 sites.
Upwork guidance: profile set-up USD 100-300, set-up and verification

## Why it works now
Profiles in Doha, Dubai and Riyadh carry reviews in Arabic and English
and most small businesses answer neither; the published price band is
USD 125-475 a month in the US and GBP 59 in the UK, and the work (a
bilingual reply within 24 hours, a weekly post, an edit check) is exactly
what an agent does without fatigue. The client grants Manager access
inside Google Business Profile, so no password changes hands. The free
10-point audit gives an honest reason to email and names faults the owner
can see on their own listing.

## What would kill it
- Google's policies on review solicitation and fake reviews: the service never writes or buys reviews, only replies to real ones and provides a compliant request kit.
- Owners who want "more 5-star reviews" rather than replies; expectations set in the first email.
- Profile suspensions need the owner's identity verification, which the agents cannot do.
- Low-cost software (USD 10-50 a month) with AI replies competes on the reply task alone; the bilingual tone and the monthly report are the difference.
