# Idea Brief: Primavera P6 baseline and update QA service (fixed fee, remote)

- **Id:** 20261025-p6-baseline-qa-service

- **Method playbook:** research/playbooks/upwork-expert-review-service.md
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## What it is
A remote, fixed-fee quality review of a contractor's P6 baseline or monthly
update: DCMA 14-point check, logic and constraint audit, float and calendar
review, a marked-up issue list and a corrected XER where agreed. Two working
days. Sold on Upwork and by direct outreach to planning managers at GCC
contractors and consultants.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Planning managers at contractors who must submit a baseline to a client or
engineer and want it to pass review; consultants who need a second pair of
eyes. USD 190 per review (price test USD 120-390); monthly repeat per project
during updates.

## Why it works now
Clients and engineers in the Gulf increasingly reject baselines on DCMA-style
grounds; small contractors have planners who build but rarely audit. The
owner's existing schedule-QA tooling turns a day's work into an hour plus
judgement, so a fixed fee below the hourly market still pays.

## What would kill it
- Owner hours (service income); mitigated by the tooling and a two-day turnaround cap
- Upwork account availability from Qatar (unverified) or a slow first contract on Upwork
- Clients unwilling to share XER files without an NDA (template ready)

## Platform and payout check (from research/platform-rules.md and management/payments.md)
