## What it is
A Gumroad pack of Excel and Google Sheets trackers in Arabic and English:
income and expenses with a dashboard, invoice tracker, cash-flow forecast and
a simple VAT-ready summary, aimed at freelancers and small shops in the GCC.
USD 19 single tracker, USD 39 bundle.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Freelancers, consultants and small shop owners who do not use accounting
software. One purchase; upsell to the automation service.

## Why it works now
Gumroad pays QAR to a Qatar bank and handles buyer taxes; template packs
cost nothing to produce with the owner's Excel skill (allowed under the
hold); the Arabic-English angle and GCC VAT summary separate the pack from
thousands of English trackers. The honest base rate is low (median creator
USD 72/month), so this is a USD 0 side product that feeds the automation
service, not a main bet.

## What would kill it
- The Gumroad median: most products sell nothing without an audience
- Free trackers everywhere; the pack must be found through the owner's outreach and groups
- Discover-sourced sales pay only 70% to the seller
