## What it is
Conversion of scanned or paper records (invoices, delivery notes, ledgers,
supplier statements, contracts, government forms) in Arabic, English or
both into verified Excel or CSV with the client's column layout, delivered
as flat-rate batches: USD 59 up to 50 pages, USD 169 up to 200 pages,
USD 390 up to 500 pages, 72-hour turnaround, agent OCR plus a bilingual
verification pass and a per-field confidence log. Sold by studio-domain
email to finance and admin managers at GCC SMEs.

## Who pays, how much, how often
Finance, admin and procurement managers at trading, contracting-supply,
clinic, school and logistics firms with paper or PDF archives feeding
accounting, audits or ERP migrations. DataConvertPro lists USD 49 / 149 /
349 flat tiers for 50 / 200 / unlimited pages with human verification;
GoTranscript from USD 3.00 per page; Upwork catalog gigs for PDF-to-Excel
from USD 10-65 with sellers at 121-1,205 reviews; XHTMLTEAM USD 12-25 per
hour for PDF, handwritten and invoice extraction. Batches recur monthly
for firms with ongoing paper flow and once for migrations and audits.

## Why it works now
The work is priced per page or per batch on three independent price
lists, the Upwork supply shows thousands of completed jobs, and the
agent cost of OCR plus verification is near zero, so the studio can match
DataConvertPro's flat tiers while adding what that market lacks: Arabic
script, mixed-direction tables and Hijri dates read correctly, with a
bilingual check. Every GCC firm doing a VAT audit, an ERP migration or an
accounting clean-up has a box of paper to turn into a sheet.

## What would kill it
- Document AI in accounting tools (Xero, Zoho Books, QuickBooks) now captures invoices for free; the service survives on odd formats, archives and Arabic handwriting.
- Confidentiality: clients send financial records; needs written terms, encrypted handling and deletion.
- Accuracy disputes on handwriting; the confidence log and a sample page before the quote limit this.
- Overlap with 20261026-python-data-extraction-service (web and digital PDF extraction for data teams); keep audiences separate: this one is finance/admin managers with scans.
