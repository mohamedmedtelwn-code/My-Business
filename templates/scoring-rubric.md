# Scoring Rubric

Score each criterion 1–5. Multiply by weight. Sum. Max 100.
Threshold to approve: 70. Owner can retune weights after the first
tuning session; record changes in the log at the bottom.

| # | Criterion | Weight | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|-----------|--------|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evidence strength | 3 | one weak source | two sources, some numbers | three+ primary sources with hard numbers |
| 2 | Capital to first dollar | 5 | > USD 500 | USD 50–200 | USD 0 (free tools only) |
| 3 | Time to first dollar | 3 | > 6 months | 1–3 months | under 2 weeks |
| 4 | Automatability | 3 | mostly owner work | half agent | agent runs it, owner presses buttons |
| 5 | Owner time per week | 2 | > 5 h | 2–5 h | under 1 h |
| 6 | Earnings ceiling at 6 months | 2 | < USD 100/mo | USD 300–1,000/mo | > USD 2,000/mo |
| 7 | Durability | 1 | one platform change kills it | some dependency | diversified, defensible |
| 8 | Legal/platform clarity | 1 | grey | mostly clear | clearly allowed, documented |

Weights sum to 20; max score 100. Capital carries the largest weight on
purpose: a free start is the default, paid starts must earn it.

## Bonuses
- +1 Durability and +1 Earnings ceiling when the idea uses an item in
  `OWNER-EDGE.md` (state which).

## Gate checks (fail any → reject before scoring)
- Illegal in operating jurisdiction or target market
- Against platform terms, or relies on deception / spam / fake reviews / impersonation
- Evidence is a single source, or only course / guru sales pages
- Requires a licence or physical presence the studio cannot provide
- No named channel and first-ten-customers path ("post and wait")

## Worked example
Faceless YouTube Shorts channel, history facts niche:
1 Evidence 4×3=12 · 2 Capital 5×5=25 · 3 Time 2×3=6 · 4 Automatability 4×3=12
· 5 Owner time 4×2=8 · 6 Ceiling 3×2=6 · 7 Durability 2×1=2 · 8 Legal 3×1=3
→ **74, approve**, flagged "slow to revenue, AI-disclosure required".
