# Owner edge

Skills, assets and markets the owner can bring to a business. This file
holds capabilities only, never personal data. Teams may use it; the
task-only rule still applies to everything else.

## Expert skills
- Construction project controls: Primavera P6 scheduling, XER file handling,
  baseline and recovery programmes, schedule QA (DCMA-style checks)
- Extension of Time (EOT) claim preparation and forensic delay analysis
  (TIA, windows, as-planned vs as-built), chronology and evidence registers
- FIDIC and Gulf public-works contract conventions
- Excel modelling, dashboards, Python tooling for schedule and cost data

## Markets the owner understands
- Construction contractors, consultants and planners in the GCC (Qatar, UAE,
  KSA); Arabic- and English-speaking engineers
- Freelance planning and claims work for small and mid-size contractors

## Assets
- Existing tooling and templates for XER processing, EOT reports, and
  schedule analysis that can be productised
- A dedicated always-on laptop for the studio

## How to use this
- Research: at least one idea per cycle must use an edge above.
- Planning: +1 on "Durability" and +1 on "Earnings ceiling" when an idea
  uses an edge; note it in the score.
- The first two cycles should weight edge-based ideas heavily: first revenue
  is most likely there.
