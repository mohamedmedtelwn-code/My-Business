## Verified from official pages (search excerpts of the official page; page not opened in full)
- Gumroad "Getting paid": bank payouts table lists **Qatar | QAR**; "If bank payouts aren't supported in your country, we will pay you via PayPal"; bank payouts take 2-7 business days; weekly payouts covering sales up to the previous Friday; a minimum payout threshold exists (amount not in excerpt). https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid
- Gumroad fees: 10% + $0.50 per direct sale, "does not include credit card processing (2.9% + $0.30)"; Discover marketplace sales 30% flat; 5% + $0.50 once paid sales reach $20,000 in a month; no monthly fee. https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees ; pricing page: "We're becoming your merchant of record". https://gumroad.com/pricing
- Gumroad does NOT pay out via Payoneer or Wise ("no wiggle room"). https://gumroad.com/help/article/223-payoneer-and-gumroad.html
- Gumroad payout settings: Gumroad uses Stripe for bank payouts; identity verification required for bank payouts; changing country clears saved bank details. https://gumroad.com/help/article/260-your-payout-settings-page.html
- Lemon Squeezy fees: platform fee 5% + 50c (help centre); payouts via Stripe: free to US banks, **1% per payout outside the US**; PayPal payouts: $0.50 US, **3% capped at $30 outside US**. https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees ; https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/help
- Lemon Squeezy getting paid: **minimum payout threshold $50**; payouts on the 14th and 28th; 1-5 days to arrive. https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/getting-paid
- Lemon Squeezy 2026: "2026 Update: Lemon Squeezy + Stripe Managed Payments" (28 Jan 2026): moving onto Stripe's merchant-of-record product. https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/2026-update  (whether new Qatar sellers are accepted under Managed Payments: UNVERIFIED)
- Wise: Qatar is on the "where do I need to live to hold money with Wise" list with no additional restriction. https://wise.com/help/articles/2813542/where-do-i-need-to-live-to-hold-money-with-wise ; business account requires a business tax ID. https://wise.com/help/articles/2897226/what-is-a-wise-account ; Wise Business card country list does not include Qatar. https://wise.com/help/articles/2935775/can-my-business-get-a-wise-card
- PayPal: paypal.com/qa exists; PayPal help: for users in Qatar a transfer to the local bank account may take up to 7 business days. https://www.paypal.com/mt/cshelp/article/how-do-i-withdraw-money-from-my-paypal-account-help394 ; PayPal policy update (effective 2025): residents of Qatar (among others) pay **3.00% to withdraw to a US bank account**. https://www.paypal.com/is/legalhub/paypal/archive-policies-full
- QNB (official): "QNB has partnered with PayPal ... withdraw funds from their PayPal wallet to their QNB account via Internet Banking"; fee table shows **PayPal withdrawal 18.20 (QAR), limit 10,000**. https://www.qnb.com.qa/sites/qnb/qnbqatar/page/en/enpaypalservices.html  (third parties say in practice only QNB works for withdrawal: https://webvator.com/paypal-qatar ; https://alidropship.com/ai-dropshipping-in-qatar/ 2026-08-29)

Stripe added **QAR SWIFT cross-border payouts (preview)** on 2026-01-28, which is a payout destination, not a Qatar merchant account. https://docs.stripe.com/changelog/clover/2026-01-28/cross-border-payouts-new-countries ; https://stripe.com/money-management/availability

## Qatar legal and tax (official and law-firm sources)
- **Law No. 9 of 2026** amending Labour Law 14/2004, published in the Official Gazette 25 June 2026: part-time and freelance workers are expressly excluded from the Labour Law's scope; the Council of Ministers may apply parts of the law or issue separate regulations for them. K&L Gates https://www.klgates.com/thought-leadership/Qatar-Introduces-Significant-Amendments-to-the-Labour-Law-9-1-2026 ; DLA Piper https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/qatar-overhauls-labour-law-to-support-flexible-work-and-strengthen-worker-protections ; Crowell https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/qatar-labor-law-key-amendments-introduced-by-law-no-9-of-2026
- As of October 2026 the implementing rules for part-time/freelance work have not been published; Qatar has no freelance visa; QFC asks sponsored individuals for a no-objection certificate from their sponsor. https://qatarsteps.com/en/guides/freelance-self-sponsored-visa (secondary, Oct 2026)
- Qatar Living (Aug 2026): an expatriate employee may work part-time for another employer through the Ministry of Labour's authorised process; businesses can be set up via MOCI, QFC or Free Zones. https://www.qatarliving.com/en/article/side-hustles-freelancing-qatar-young-people
- **MOCI E-Commerce Licence (Decision No. 25 of 2026)**: operate e-commerce activities entirely online; 194 approved activities; issuance fee **QAR 500**; applications online. https://www.moci.gov.qa/en/news/554  (who is eligible — company vs individual, Qatari vs resident — not in excerpt: UNVERIFIED)
- MOCI Home Licence via Hukoomi: "local investors" may apply; fee **QAR 1,020**. https://hukoomi.gov.qa/en/services/apply-for-a-home-license-ministry-of-commerce-and-industry
- MOCI FAQ: a person may establish only one sole proprietorship; GCC citizens may practise business in Qatar; whether non-GCC residents can hold a sole-proprietorship CR is not stated (UNVERIFIED; Qatar Living says Qataris/GCC only).  https://www.moci.gov.qa/en/our-services/investor/commerce-faq/establishing-companies
- Tax: Income Tax Law 24/2018: 10% on Qatar-source taxable income; salaries, wages and allowances are not taxed; "a self-employed individual may be subject to income tax if one derives qualifying income from sources in Qatar" (PwC, 17 Sep 2026). https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/Qatar/Individual/Taxes-on-personal-income ; GTA: every taxpayer carrying on an activity must register (Dhareeba). https://gta.gov.qa/en/laws ; https://gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Law%20No.%20(24)%20of%202018%20Promulgating%20the%20Income%20Tax%20Law%20(3).pdf
- Local gateways (Dibsy, SkipCash, Sadad, QPay) CR requirements: not retrieved (UNVERIFIED).
