## Category 7: Local physical

Evidence found (all via search excerpts):
- Industry-level: a secondhand citation of the NAMA census says 2023 US vending sales were about $18.2B across about 2.9M machines, about $6,284 per machine per year, about $524 per month. I could not tell which of the vmfsusa.com / ogscapital.com pages carried it, and I did not see NAMA's own page. 2023 data, not 2025.
- Operator/vendor ranges (gross revenue per machine per month): low traffic $300-700; moderate $800-1,500; high $1,500-3,000+. A separate vending guide says $300-600 net per month for a well-placed snack and beverage machine. These come from machine sellers (vmfsusa.com), so treat as sales-driven.
- Share of operators earning meaningful income: no distribution data found.
- Time to first dollar: no sourced figure.
- GCC/Qatar vending, delivery, cleaning, events, kiosk economics: not found (never searched successfully; search budget). UNVERIFIED.

Qatar licensing and resident feasibility:
- VERIFIED (MOCI excerpts): Law 1 of 2019 lets foreign investors own up to 100% in permitted sectors; excluded are banks and insurance, commercial agencies, real-estate purchase and other listed sectors. A non-Qatari investor must open a bank account in Qatar for the project. MOCI registration involves a beneficial-owner declaration. A MOCI-hosted guide (file path dated 2026-05) says Single Window takes 2 days and costs QAR 2,200.
- CONFLICT: a Qatar Living guide says only Qataris and GCC nationals can set up an individual establishment (sole proprietorship). MOCI's own pages, as excerpted, are ambiguous (the FAQ says the owner must be a natural person; one person may hold only one sole proprietorship). Treat "an expat can register a sole proprietorship" as UNVERIFIED and probably no. The realistic evidenced route is an LLC/other form with MOCI activity approval.
- Consultancy sources (emerhub, wafeq, deel) say a commercial licence, municipal approval, sector permits and a GTA tax card (within 30 days) follow the CR. Consultancies, so secondary.
- Can an employee-visa resident legally run this? UNVERIFIED. Law 21 of 2015 (job changes need ministry approval, same field, with exceptions) was confirmed in general terms, but I did not find the text of any article on outside business or side work. Law 9 of 2026 (Labour Law amendments) adds a part-time/freelance framework, but sources conflict: one says the group is outside the Labour Law, another says it is now formally recognised; detailed conditions come in later ministerial decisions; no source established a second-job permission rule. Effective date conflicts (about 1 July vs 25 July 2026).
- Entrepreneur Residency (self-sponsored, needs incubator endorsement) and a "Mustaqel" visa were mentioned in secondary sources only. UNVERIFIED.

## Category 8: Affiliate and lead-gen

Traffic and AI Overviews (primary-ish):
- Ahrefs (re-run, Dec 2025 vs Dec 2023, 300,000 keywords, desktop GSC data, correlational): AI Overview presence goes with 58% lower position-1 CTR. Informational position-1 CTR fell from 0.076 to 0.039; for AI-Overview keywords from 0.073 to 0.016. The earlier Ahrefs study (Mar 2024 vs Mar 2025) found 34.5%.
- Secondary: Seer Interactive via roundups: organic CTR on AI-Overview queries fell 61% (1.76% to 0.61%, Jun 2024 to Sep 2025). Pew (Jul 2025): 8% clicked a link with an AI Overview vs 15% without. Chartbeat via Press Gazette (through GetChatAds): Google search referrals to publishers down 33% globally in 2025 (US 38%). Affiverse (via secondary): 71% of monitored affiliate sites lost rankings after the March 2026 core update; another source attributes a similar 71% figure to a December 2025 update, so the update is unresolved.
- AI Overview coverage estimates vary from about 10% to 55% of queries depending on tracker; Flippa says about 20% of informational queries.

Earnings distribution (weak, course-seller or vendor sources):
- Authority Hacker survey (2,270 affiliates, 2024-2025, reported by revshare.so): mean $8,038/month; 81.2% under $20,000/year; 3.78% over $150,000/year. Authority Hacker sells courses, so respondents are a self-selected audience. A review says the founders discontinued Authority Hacker because the niche-site model is no longer worth it (unconfirmed on their own site).
- Productive Blogging 2026 survey (via jobsafterai.com): median year-one earnings $4.44/month; survivorship bias means the true median is lower.
- Lead-gen for local services, directories, comparison tools: no earnings data found (UNVERIFIED).

Site-sale multiples (brokers have a commercial interest):
- Empire Flippers (mid-2025 post): average content-site multiple about 24x monthly profit (about 2 years of annual profit), 27x in 2024, about 30x in 2023. Share of asking price achieved 85% (2024) to 82% (2025). Content sites sold: about 70 (2023), about 50 (2024), about 30 by mid-2025. Another EF page states about 25x monthly net profit (page not identified). EF scoreboard: 26.4x TTM average for typical businesses, 28.5x premium (undated, all categories).
- Empire Flippers moved all multiples to annual (post dated 8 Sep 2026 per excerpt), so 24x monthly is about 2.0x annual.
- Flippa: content sites 2.85x profit and 2.8x revenue; category volume down 37% (H1 2025); 2025 review says content sites down 33.5%; $10K-$100K band averages 1.8x profit, $1M+ band 2.9x; top quartile 5.23x; "traffic cliff" for generic Q&A content. INFERENCE: 2.85x annual profit is about 34x monthly, so the Flippa and EF figures are roughly consistent once the basis is aligned, with small sites at the low end (1.8x annual = about 22x monthly).
- Jean Galea's guide claims 28-38x monthly for diversified sites; unsourced, low confidence.
- Motion Invest data: the search returned nothing relevant. UNVERIFIED.

Qatar feasibility: UNVERIFIED for licensing of an online content or lead-gen business, for affiliate-network payout availability, and for the side-income question (see Category 7 for Law 9/2026).

## Category 9: Communities and memberships

Patreon:
- VERIFIED primary: standard 10% platform fee for pages published after 4 Aug 2025; legacy plans stay at 5% (Founders), 8% (Pro), 11% (Pro + Merch) unless the page is unpublished. Processing on the standard plan: 2.9% + $0.30 for cards/Apple Pay and US PayPal/Venmo; 3.9% + $0.30 for non-US PayPal/Venmo. The same platform fee applies to one-time purchases. One secondary source (AlternativeTo) says 13% for former Premium; Patreon's own excerpt says 11%, so use 11%.
- Distribution (old): Graphtreon estimate reported Feb 2019: over 4,300 of 132,500 active creators at $1,000+/month (3.2%). Regner (2020): 25% of active campaigns over $120/month, 5% over $750, 1% over $2,500. Graphtreon mean payout about $126/month (via Jobreeze). No 2025-26 distribution found; Patreon does not appear to publish one. Patreon's claim that over 90% of highly engaged creators earn $1,000+ is platform-reported and correlational.

Skool:
- No official revenue data. Third-party trackers: profitable.app says about 70% of communities are free; among paid, median about $1,217/month (price x members, an upper bound assuming no churn), just over half above $1,000. RevenueGeeks: median trending paid group 205 members at about $27/month, about $5,500 gross; trending-list survivorship. Skool Games leaderboard claims are unverified.
- Pricing (from a competitor blog, schoolmaker.com, and affiliate-style reviews; Skool's page not seen): Hobby $9/month + 10% + $0.30; Pro $99/month + about 2.9% + $0.30 (conflicting detail on a 1% fee above about $900). Break-even Hobby vs Pro about $1,200-1,400/month.

Whop:
- Self-reported: average $8,413/month per earning creator (mean, skewed); over $845M lifetime creator earnings (Oct 2025); 258 sellers over $1M by mid-2025 out of over 200,000 sellers. Sacra: 183,628 sellers and 14.2M users (Feb 2026); about $142M annualised Whop revenue (Oct 2025, Sacra estimate). Foundry review: about 1% of products earn over half of revenue and about 2% clear $1,000/month. Payout totals conflict across sources ($400M vs $3B).

Discord paid servers, cohort-based courses (Maven, etc.): not searched. UNVERIFIED.

Time to first dollar: no sourced figure for any platform. Month-6 median: no direct data; best proxies are the Patreon distribution (old) and Skool/Whop estimates above.

Qatar: payout country support for Patreon, Stripe/Skool, Whop for Qatar residents UNVERIFIED (not checked).

## Category 10: Finance-adjacent

- Qatar Central Bank (2018): instructed banks and exchange houses not to deal in bitcoin (reported by Qatar Tribune, The Peninsula 7 Feb 2018, and an Ahli Bank news page). Reporting differs on whether individuals face penalties; the circular targets institutions. The QCB circular itself was not found.
- QFCRA: authorised firms may not provide virtual asset services or deal in virtual assets "until further notice" (alert PDF on qfcra.com; date described as 2019 by the QFCRA-domain excerpt, Jan 2020 by The Block, Dec 2020 by a law-firm page). In 2024 QFCRA clarified that cryptocurrencies, stablecoins and CBDCs are "Excluded Tokens" under the QFC Digital Assets Regulations 2024 and the earlier restrictions remain.
- QFC Digital Assets Framework 2024 covers tokenised securities/regulated security tokens, not crypto. QCB CBDC work is wholesale only (qcb.gov.qa digital currency page).
- Staking/yield from Qatar: no legal source says an individual is committing an offence by holding crypto on an offshore exchange; no source says it is permitted. UNVERIFIED. The binding constraint evidenced is the banking channel (banks barred from dealing).
- Tax: one 2026 guide says no specific crypto tax legislation; another promotional piece claims a framework; unreliable.
- Cash-back stacking, bank account bonuses, high-yield arbitrage, Qatari bank deposit rates, whether any Qatar bank pays sign-up bonuses: not searched. UNVERIFIED. No earnings evidence for any finance-adjacent method.
- Evidence type: legal/regulatory commentary; law-firm and fintech-vendor blogs (Lightspark, Freeman Law) are secondary.
