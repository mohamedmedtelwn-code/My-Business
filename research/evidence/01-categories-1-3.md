# Categories 1-3 evidence

Research date: 2026-10-25. Scope: content platforms, digital products, marketplaces.

**Method limits (read first).** WebFetch failed on every host (DNS: ENOTFOUND), and curl through the agent proxy was refused (403) for sec.gov, revenuecat.com and ppc.land. I did not work around that. The shared WebSearch budget ran out after about 28 searches. Every figure below therefore comes from search-engine result summaries, not from pages I opened. Where a number came from a primary-source URL (Etsy 10-K, Substack, Spotify) I still only saw it through a summary, so I cap confidence at M unless two sources agree or the arithmetic cross-checks. Items marked UNVERIFIED must not be used as fact. "Derived" means my own arithmetic on sourced inputs.

## Category 1: Content platforms

### YouTube (long-form and Shorts)
- **YPP thresholds (third-party sources, not Google's page):** ad revenue share needs 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. A lower tier at 500 subscribers (plus 3 public uploads in 90 days) unlocks fan funding and Shopping only. Shorts watch hours do not count toward the 4,000 hours. [5]
- **Revenue share:** 55% to creators on long-form, 45% on Shorts (pooled), applies at the ad-revenue tier. [5][6]
- **Reported future change, UNVERIFIED:** search summaries say the Tier 2 bar rises on 1 Feb 2027 to 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views. I could not confirm this on Google's own page. Verify before relying on it. [5]
- **Inauthentic content policy (15 Jul 2025):** YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" and strengthened detection of mass-produced, templated content. YouTube's Rene Ritchie described it as a clarification. AI-assisted content stays eligible if it adds real value. Outlets disagree on whether it is a tightening or a clarification. [4]
- **Platform totals:** YouTube said at Made on YouTube (Sept 2025) it had paid more than $100B to creators, artists and media partners over four years. The last YPP member count I found is about 3M (2023); no 2025 count found. [10]
- **Shorts RPM:** vidIQ puts most creators at $0.03-$0.10 per 1,000 Shorts views after YouTube's cut, with most channels at $0.03-$0.06 (about $30-$60 per 1M views). AIR claims a ceiling of about $0.20-$0.33. Long-form RPM for most creators is $1-$10. vidIQ and AIR sell creator tools, so treat them as vendor estimates. [6]
- **Time to first dollar / base rate:** vidIQ (Sept 2026) analysed 32.9M channels that had under 1,000 subscribers at the start. Within 360 days, 1.86% reached 1,000 subs with 1-11 uploads, 8.65% with 12-51 uploads, and 30.77% with 52+ uploads. The study is observational and vidIQ is a vendor. [7] Scalelab states an average of 254 days to 1,000 subs, with no stated method and a mean rather than a median. [8]
- **Income reports:** faceless-channel guides give $20-$200/mo in months 3-4 and $200-$1,500/mo in months 5-8 (optimistic); a more conservative guide gives $0-$200/mo for months 3-6. One first-person report showed a first AdSense payout of about GBP 41 in month 4 and about GBP 380/mo by month 7, with most income from a sponsored video and consulting. I cannot map this report to a specific URL among the results. These are SEO/vendor pages (several sell AI video tools). Flag as guru/vendor-heavy. [9]
- **Synthesis (not sourced):** at month 6 a median new channel earns $0 because it has not cleared YPP. Channels that did clear it earn low hundreds at most.

### TikTok
- **Creator Rewards eligibility:** 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in the last 30 days, original videos at least 1 minute. All from third-party blogs; TikTok does not publish a standard RPM. [11]
- **RPM:** $0.40-$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views; qualified views are reportedly 40-70% of total views. Derived: roughly $160-$700 per 1M total views. High-RPM niches reportedly $1.50-$2.00. No real small-creator payout data found. [11]
- **Brand deals dominate:** a guide citing Influencer Marketing Hub 2025 says 84% of creators earning over $10k/mo make most of it from brand deals. I could not verify the original. [11]

### Podcasts
- **Downloads (Buzzsprout via Podderapp, July 2026):** median episode gets 27 downloads in its first 7 days. Top 25% is 97, top 10% is 409, top 5% is 1,010, and top 1% is 4,579. These are aggregator transcriptions; older pages cite slightly different Buzzsprout 2025 figures. [14]
- **Spotify Partner Program:** the creator share is 50% of ad revenue from Spotify-monetised ads. A third-party blog lists eligibility as 3 published episodes, 2,000 consumption hours and 1,000 audience in the last 30 days. I could not confirm those exact criteria on Spotify's page, and they may differ from the live requirements. [14]
- **Time to first dollar:** no data found. A median new show with 27 downloads/week is far below any programme threshold, so month-6 revenue is about $0 absent sponsorship.

### Newsletters (Substack, beehiiv)
- **Substack:** announced 5M paid subscriptions in March 2025 (up from 4M in Nov 2024). These are subscriptions, not unique payers; total active subscriptions were above 35M at the time. [12]
- **Writer revenue:** gross writer revenue is reported at about $450M for 2025 vs about $375M in 2024; Substack's 10% commission is about $45M (secondary, internally consistent). [12]
- **Distribution claims, UNVERIFIED:** a $850 median annual income attributed to a Tow Center study and "12% of writers have active paying subscribers" appear in an SEO aggregator with no primary source found. Free-to-paid conversion is commonly cited as about 3%. [12]
- **beehiiv (self-published):**
  - Median free-to-paid conversion is 0.62%.
  - Median time to first dollar for new newsletters is 66 days (State of Newsletters 2026, as cited by a third party).
  - Company revenue was about $28M in 2025; paid-subscription revenue was about $19M in 2025 vs about $8M in 2024.
  - The ad network pays more than $1M/mo to publishers.
  - Creators with diversified revenue earn about 3x more (self-reported).
  - Sources conflict on totals ($25M+ vs $28M). [13]
- **Derived month-6 range:** at a 0.62% median conversion, 1,000 free subscribers is about 6 paying readers, or about $30-$60/mo at an assumed $5-$10/mo price. The price is my assumption.

### Share earning meaningfully (creator economy general)
- Linktree 2022 (older): 46% of creators earned under $1,000 a year; 12% of full-time creators earned over $50k. [16]
- 2025 NeoReach tally: more than 68% earned under $50k. Goldman estimate of about 4% "professional" (over $100k) is secondary only. [16]

### Evidence-quality flag for Category 1
Mixed. Platform-level totals are primary, but they hide medians. Per-operator income evidence (YouTube/TikTok RPM, faceless income reports, newsletter "how to make money" posts) is mostly written by tool vendors (vidIQ, AIR, clipspeed) or by beehiiv and Substack themselves. Treat it as optimistic.

## Category 2: Digital products

### Ebooks / Amazon KDP
- **Written Word Media 2025 Indie Author Survey (n over 1,300):** 44% earn $100/mo or less. 56% earn more than $100. About 20% earn $500-$5,000/mo, 13% earn over $5,000/mo, and 8% earn over $10,000/mo. WWM sells marketing services, and the sample skews to engaged authors, so it overstates the median KDP author. [15]
- **ALLi 2025 Indie Author Income Survey:** a $13,500 median is reported only by secondary sites. I did not find ALLi's own results page, so it is UNVERIFIED. The sample is limited to authors who spend over half their time or draw over half their income from writing, so it is not a newcomer median. [15]
- **Below the survey populations:** an editing-service blog says about 40% of authors with at least one book earn under $500/yr, citing unnamed surveys (UNVERIFIED). Another roundup says 75% earn under $1,000/yr with no source. [15]
- **AI rules:** KDP requires disclosure of AI-generated text, images and translations. AI-assisted editing, brainstorming and grammar checks generally do not need disclosure. Third-party guides describe sweeps and takedowns. The Authors Guild reported a KDP daily upload cap (2023); the "3 titles per day" figure rests on one guide and is UNVERIFIED. [15]
- **Time to first dollar / month-6:** no usable data. Derived reading of WWM: roughly 44% sit at or below $100/mo even among engaged authors.

### Templates (Notion etc.), printables, Lightroom presets, fonts
- **Notion templates:** SEO and vendor guides give $500-$5,000/mo for "active sellers" and up to $50,000+ for top sellers. There is no audited or platform-published distribution. These are mostly vendor/course content. [18]
- **Printables, Lightroom presets, fonts:** no primary or credible distribution data found. UNVERIFIED. Etsy-digital blog estimates are the only proxy.
- **Etsy digital-download blogs:** new shops $50-$200/mo; established $500-$5,000+. A median active Etsy shop is said to make GBP 100-250/mo gross. Another guide claims $2,000-$10,000/mo in 6-12 months with a 200+ product catalog, which is promotional. I could not map each figure to a specific page among the results returned. [20]

### Courses
- GetResponse survey of 1,000 course creators (undated, possibly pre-2025): 93% earn some income and the top 8% earn over $10k/mo. [19]
- Ruzuku (platform vendor, 2026): 32,000+ courses and about $78.9M creator revenue (period not stated). Derived: about $2,470 per course on average, a mean skewed by top courses. [19]
- Kajabi's 2025 State of Creator Commerce is referenced, but I did not see its earnings figures. UNVERIFIED.

### Gumroad
- No official 2025 median or creator-earnings report found. Sacra: flat 10% fee since 2023; 2024 revenue about $21M (Latka estimates $23.8M); sources conflict on GMV trend. A "$1B lifetime payouts" claim comes from a SWOT sales page and is UNVERIFIED. [17]

### Evidence-quality flag for Category 2
Mostly course-sellers, SEO blogs and tool vendors. The only author-run data are the WWM and ALLi surveys, and both skew to serious authors. No primary Gumroad, Notion or Creative Market distribution was found.

## Category 3: Marketplaces

### Etsy (FY2025 10-K, filed early 2026)
- **Sellers:** 8,762K total active sellers at year-end 2025 (includes Depop), up 7.7% from 8,134K. Etsy marketplace had 5.6M, down 1.5% YoY. Depop had 3.2M, up 41.1%. An active seller has had a charge or sale in the last 12 months. [1][2]
- **GMS:** total $11,916.9M in 2025 vs $12,586.95M in 2024 (-5.3%). Etsy marketplace $10,460.7M, Depop $1,074.9M, Reverb $381.3M. The components sum to the total. Another source gives 2024 GMS as $12.72B, likely a restatement or scope difference (reason UNVERIFIED). [1][2][20]
- **Derived GMS per active seller:** Etsy marketplace about $1,868/yr, or about $156/mo, as a mean, before fees, shipping and cost of goods, and with the median far lower. Depop about $336/yr. Neither is a figure stated in the filing. [1]
- **Seller census 2024 (n=2,402, via secondary summary):** 89% one-person businesses, 97% home-based, 29% say the creative business is their sole occupation, and it contributes 11% of household income on average. Etsy publishes no current distribution of seller earnings. [20]
- **Fees (third-party blogs, not Etsy's page):** $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction, US payment processing 3% + $0.25, Offsite Ads 12-15% on attributed sales (cap $100/order). Offsite Ads rate tiers conflict across sources. [20]
- **Policy:** Creativity Standards launched July 2024 and were revised 10 Jun 2025. Sellers must disclose AI use in the listing. Reported bars cover unmodified Canva templates or stock graphics and AI images without meaningful creative input. Etsy's House Rules page exists, but I saw only a summary. A reported 11 Aug 2026 change on laser-cut and sublimation items rests on one commentary source. The "17,000 listings removed" claim has no primary source. [20]
- **Time to first sale:** Etsy community threads (anecdotes) range from days to 4 months, clustering around 2 weeks. No official median. [20]

### Apple App Store / Google Play
- **RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026** (115,000+ apps, over $16B revenue, FY2025 data): new subscription-app launches rose from about 2,000/mo to almost 15,000/mo. Top 25% of apps grew MRR 80% YoY, top 10% grew 306%, bottom quartile fell 33%, and the median grew about 5%. [21]
- **Share reaching $1K/mo:** a secondary blog attributes to RevenueCat 2026 that about 83% of new apps stay under $1,000/mo within two years and 4.6% reach $10K/mo. The 2025 report, as relayed by a RevenueCat employee on HackerNoon, said about 17% ever reach $1K/mo, 3.5% reach $10K/mo, and the median subscription app makes under $50/mo at its first anniversary. I could not open the report. [21]
- **Selection bias:** RevenueCat's panel covers subscription apps that integrate its SDK and monetise. The true share of all published apps earning anything is lower.
- **Market totals:** Sensor Tower puts 2025 global in-app purchase revenue at $167B (+10.6%), and Appfigures puts consumer spend at $155.8B. These are market totals, not per-developer data. Modelled per-app estimates carry 5-25% median absolute error. [21]
- **AI apps:** reportedly earn 41% more per customer but churn 30% faster (RevenueCat via ppc.land). [21]
- **Commission programmes (15% small-business rates for Apple and Google):** UNVERIFIED in this session; not covered by any result I received.

### Shopify App Store
- **RevenueHunt estimate (modelled from public pricing, not billing data):** top 1% of apps capture about 72% of estimated revenue; only about 36% of apps appear to earn anything; median review count is 5, and four in five apps have under 10 reviews. [22]
- **2021 Store Leads data (old):** median app revenue $725/mo, mean $20,037/mo, and median $0 for apps under three months old. [22]
- **App counts:** 14,600+ (Store Leads) vs 17,600+ (April 2026) — sources conflict. [22]
- **Revenue share:** a claim of 0% on the first $1M and 15% above it is UNVERIFIED. [22]

### Gumroad / Creative Market
- Gumroad: see Category 2. Creative Market: the only commission figures are from a competitor's marketing page (40-50% non-exclusive, 30% exclusive) and are UNVERIFIED. No seller-earnings data found. [17][23]

### Evidence-quality flag for Category 3
Etsy figures are primary (SEC filing). App-store data is vendor-published aggregate data with selection bias. Shopify, Gumroad and Creative Market data are third-party estimates or competitor marketing. "Income report" content for Etsy is dominated by SEO blogs and AI-income promotion.
