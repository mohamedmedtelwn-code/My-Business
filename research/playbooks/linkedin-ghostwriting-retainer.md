# Playbook: LinkedIn ghostwriting retainer

Checked 2026-10-26. Sources in the brief 20261026-gcc-linkedin-ghostwriting.

## What is sold
A monthly retainer: one 30-minute call, 4-8 posts a month in the client's
voice (English, Arabic or both), a profile rewrite in month one, a monthly
one-page report. Entry price USD 500-700 for 4 posts, USD 900-1,300 for 8.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Write three sample posts for a public founder from their own interviews;
   use them as the outreach proof.
2. List 20 founders of recently funded GCC startups and partners of small
   consultancies whose profiles post less than monthly.
3. Message each with one sample post written for them and the entry offer.
4. Onboarding: voice questionnaire, 10 past posts or talks, topics list.
5. Monthly call recorded; agent drafts posts from the transcript; owner
   edits for voice; client approves in a shared doc; client schedules or
   gives posting access.
6. Report impressions and inbound replies from the client's analytics
   screenshots; use the numbers to move the client to the mid tier.
7. Fiverr gig in parallel at USD 150-300 per 4-post pack.

## Prices observed
Entry USD 500-1,500/month (4-8 posts); mid USD 1,500-3,500; per post USD
50-150 entry, 150-300 mid; one Fiverr test gig: 47 orders, USD 4,200 net in
90 days.

## Tools
Call recorder and transcript, shared doc for approvals, LinkedIn analytics
screenshots, Fiverr.

## Time per week
Owner 1-1.5 h per client; capacity 4-6 clients.

## Ramp
First dollar 2-4 weeks by outreach. At 6 months: USD 1,000-4,000 a month
with 2-4 retainers. Median unknown.

## Risks
Generic AI content lowers reach and churns clients; clients expect leads;
owner editing time caps growth.
