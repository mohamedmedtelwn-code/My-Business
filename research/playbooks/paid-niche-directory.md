# Playbook: paid niche directory with featured listings

Checked 2026-11-02. Sources in the brief 20261102-arabic-saas-directory.

## What is sold
Free verified listings for every tool in a narrow niche; revenue from
featured placement (USD 79 a month or USD 790 a year per category),
sponsored comparison pages, and affiliate links where vendors run
programmes that pay to Qatar.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Pick one search intent the site can own ("Arabic-ready software");
   write the verification checklist.
2. Build a static bilingual site on a studio sub-domain; 50+ listings
   verified on vendor free trials before any outreach.
3. Email 20 then 30 vendor marketing managers (company addresses): claim
   the free listing, upgrade to featured; opt-out line.
4. Publish comparison pages for each category; add the directory link to
   every deliverable of the studio's other services.
5. Sell featured listings once the site sends measurable clicks; add
   sponsored pages and pay-per-lead when traffic justifies it.

## Prices observed
OpenAlternative USD 197/month featured (35% of USD 6,500 MRR, about
70,000 monthly visitors, about a year to meaningful revenue);
JobBoardSearch USD 100K+ lifetime over 4 years; a UK directory
GBP 1,200/month premium listings plus GBP 400 featured; AI Directories
about USD 8,270 trailing-30-day from one-time submission packages;
IndieNeed USD 250 total at launch; Frey Chu USD 2,500/month recurring.

## Tools
Static site generator in the repo, GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages
(free), the studio email sender, Gumroad subscription product.

## Time per week
Owner 0 h after one DNS record.

## Ramp
First dollar 6-12 weeks. At 6 months: USD 0-800 a month; the published
cases reach USD 2,500-6,500 only after a year or more.

## Risks
Slow traffic; organic search cannot be the only channel; directory
saturation and AI answers; verification burden.
