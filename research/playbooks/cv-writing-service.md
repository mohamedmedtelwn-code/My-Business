# Playbook: bilingual CV and LinkedIn package for a regional job market

Observed method: resume writers charge a median USD 42/h on Upwork (USD 25-65)
and USD 100-2,500 per service (Fiverr guide); one Fiverr seller shows 15,400
orders; Gulf-focused services charge QAR 249 for an Arabic-English translation
alone and USD 19-89 per add-on. Sources: brief 20261025-gcc-bilingual-cv-service.

## Steps
1. Write a questionnaire that captures roles, achievements with numbers and target jobs.
2. Agent drafts the English CV (ATS-safe layout), the Arabic version, LinkedIn text and a cover letter; owner reviews.
3. Ask 20 job seekers in the owner's network directly, at the test price; collect three before-and-after examples (anonymised) for the listing.
4. List on Fiverr and Upwork; add a free GCC CV checklist as a lead magnet.
5. Deliver in three working days; one revision; ask for a review.

## Tools
Word, a free ATS checker, PayPal invoicing via QNB or Gumroad.

## Time and ramp
- Time to first dollar: under 2 weeks through direct asks.
- Typical monthly range at 6 months: USD 300-1,500 at 4-20 orders a month.

## Kill signals
Zero payments from 20 asks; review score under 4.5; more than 30 owner minutes per CV.
