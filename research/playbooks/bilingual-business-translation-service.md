# Playbook: Arabic-English business translation with native review

Checked 2026-10-26. Sources in the brief 20261026-arabic-english-business-translation.

## What is sold
Per-word translation and localisation of business content (web pages,
proposals, decks, app strings, social posts) with an agent draft and a
native bilingual review. Not certified or legal translation.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Pick two niches with recurring content (SaaS entering the GCC, clinics
   and training providers in Qatar).
2. Make a 300-word sample of a prospect's own homepage in Gulf Arabic; send
   it with a per-word quote (USD 0.10-0.14 general, 0.18-0.25 specialised,
   under the market's USD 0.13-0.30 band to win first jobs).
3. 20 outreach messages a week; follow up on day 3.
4. Profiles on Upwork and ProZ with the samples; answer Arabic-English posts
   daily; Fiverr outcome gig "landing page in Gulf Arabic, 48 h".
5. Per job: glossary first, agent draft, owner review for register and
   terminology, QA pass for numbers, dates and RTL layout, delivery.
6. Keep a translation memory per client; quote repeats at 50%.
7. Decline certified, court and attestation work (MOJ-approved offices only).

## Prices observed
English-Arabic USD 0.13-0.30/word general, 0.22-0.50 specialised (12-source
aggregate); Arabic-English USD 0.14-0.22 general; USD 280-440 per 2,000
words; Gulf platforms USD 0.05-0.15; top Upwork profiles USD 15-20/h with
248-310 jobs.

## Tools
Agent translation draft, glossary and TM files, Upwork, ProZ, Fiverr.

## Time per week
Owner 4-6 h review at 2,000-10,000 words a month.

## Ramp
First dollar 1-3 weeks by outreach, 4-8 weeks on platforms. At 6 months:
USD 300-1,500 a month. Median unknown.

## Risks
Price pressure from raw machine translation; certified work out of scope;
income scales with owner review hours.
