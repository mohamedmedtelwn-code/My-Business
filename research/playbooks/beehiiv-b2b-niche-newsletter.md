# Playbook: B2B niche newsletter on beehiiv, sponsor-funded first

Observed method: publishers curate one niche weekly, grow a free list, sell
sponsor placements at 2.5-5% of subscriber count per placement (Paved, 2026) or
USD 6.53-23 CPM, then add a paid tier converting a median 0.62% of readers
(beehiiv, 2026). Median time to first dollar on beehiiv is 66 days.

## Steps
1. Pick a niche with weekly news and professional readers (here GCC project controls).
2. Publish weekly on beehiiv's free plan; every issue has one owned asset (template, tip).
3. Grow by appending the sign-up link to every outreach message of the studio's other businesses and by weekly posts in the readers' groups.
4. At 1,000 subscribers, pitch 10 sponsors from the niche's vendors with a one-page rate card (USD 25-50 per placement at 1,000; USD 250-500 at 10,000).
5. Add a paid tier only once a Qatar-compatible payout exists.

## Tools
beehiiv (free to 2,500 subscribers; Pro USD 95/month for direct sponsorships), LinkedIn, public tender and news pages.

## Time and ramp
- Time to first dollar: 3-5 months here (sponsors need about 1,000 readers).
- Typical monthly range at 6 months: USD 0-150.

## Kill signals
Under 300 subscribers at day 90; open rate under 30%; no sponsor reply from 10 pitches.
