# Playbook: niche technical course on Udemy with a YouTube funnel

Observed method: practitioners record a technical course once and sell it on
Udemy. Udemy pays 97% on sales through the instructor's own coupon links and
37% on sales Udemy brings (support.udemy.com, read 2026-10-25); payouts via
PayPal or Payoneer, USD 25 minimum. The top English P6 course shows 51,758
students; the biggest Arabic one 3,097; a vendor roundup says 75% of
instructors earn under USD 1,000 a year.

## Steps
1. Pick a topic where English courses are large and the target language's are small or stale.
2. Write the curriculum and exercises (agent); record 4-8 hours (owner).
3. Publish on Udemy; create a 97%-share coupon.
4. Post the first 2-3 modules free on YouTube with the coupon link; post weekly tips in community groups.
5. Collect reviews early (first 20 learners via outreach lists); update yearly.

## Tools
OBS (free), P6 or Excel for screen capture, Udemy, YouTube.

## Time and ramp
- Build: 30-40 owner hours of recording plus agent drafting.
- Time to first dollar: 1-3 months.
- Typical monthly range at 6 months: USD 50-500; median instructor earnings are low, owner-driven traffic is what moves the number.

## Kill signals
Under 20 sales in the first 90 days; review score under 4.3; a free equivalent with more views.
