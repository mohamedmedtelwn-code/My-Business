# Playbook: fixed-fee tender and proposal writing

Checked 2026-11-05. Sources in the brief 20261105-gcc-bid-proposal-writing-service.

## What is sold
Bilingual tender responses and proposals structured to the evaluation
criteria, with a compliance matrix and method statement: USD 390 single
proposal, USD 890 full tender file, USD 190 capability statement. No
submission on the client's behalf, no compliance guarantee.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Build the templates: compliance matrix, method statement, capability
   statement, pricing schedule, bilingual layout.
2. List suppliers from public award notices and registers (non-construction
   categories while the owner hold runs); share the list with the
   tender-digest test, one email per firm per month.
3. Email 20 then 30 naming one live tender the firm qualifies for and
   offering a free compliance matrix for it; opt-out line.
4. Quote fixed; collect the firm's material by a short questionnaire;
   draft, two review rounds, deliver 3-5 days before the deadline.
5. Delete the material after the submission date; offer the next bid.

## Prices observed
Labeeb (GCC): AED 2,500 / 4,500 / 7,500 per document. Upwork tender
freelancers USD 500-3,000 per project; profiles at USD 10-60/h with
USD 10K-200K lifetime. UK: junior GBP 500-1,500 per bid, day rates
GBP 350-800.

## Tools
Document tooling in the runner, the tender-digest crawler for live
opportunities, the studio email sender, Gumroad for the capability
statement.
