# Playbook: Google Sheets add-on (and Excel add-in) micro-SaaS

Checked 2026-10-26. Sources in the brief 20261026-tafqeet-invoice-sheets-addon.

## What is sold
A small utility inside Google Sheets (Workspace Marketplace, 0% fee) with a
free tier and a Pro licence key sold through Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy;
optionally the same function as an Excel .xlam add-in sold on Gumroad.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Pick one function users already ask for in forums and that free scripts
   get wrong (evidence: tafqeet grammar and fractions).
2. Build the Apps Script add-on in 1-2 weeks with a free quota and a
   licence-key check against Gumroad's licence API.
3. Google Cloud project, OAuth consent screen (minimal scopes), privacy
   policy on a domain, Marketplace listing with a 60-second video; expect
   2-4 weeks of review.
4. Meanwhile sell the Excel .xlam and the templates on Gumroad from week 2.
5. Launch where the question is asked: accounting and bookkeeping groups,
   Stack Overflow and Reddit threads, a free web converter page linking to
   the add-on.
6. Give 20 bookkeepers the Pro key free for reviews on the listing.
7. Track installs to paid; lifetime deals convert better than monthly for
   utilities (Email-to-Sheets: subscriptions plus lifetime deals).

## Numbers observed
Sync2Sheets USD 9,000 MRR, 400+ paying from 40,000 installs (~1%);
Email-to-Sheets USD 1,500/month, 60 customers after two years; ForecastSheets
3,000 users, USD 0 (no paid tier, no distribution beyond search); median
indie app under USD 50/month.

## Tools
Apps Script, Gumroad licence keys, a domain, GitHub for the engine and tests.

## Time per week
Owner 1-2 h after launch.

## Ramp
First dollar 4-8 weeks. At 6 months: USD 0-300 a month is the realistic
band for a utility without an audience; outliers reach thousands.

## Risks
Free alternatives, OAuth review delays, platform adding the feature natively.
