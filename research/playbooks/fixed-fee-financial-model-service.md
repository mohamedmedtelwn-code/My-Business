# Playbook: fixed-fee financial models for startups and SMEs

Checked 2026-10-26. Sources in the brief 20261026-startup-financial-model-service.

## What is sold
An investor-ready three-statement Excel model: assumptions tab, revenue
drivers, headcount and cost schedules, income statement, balance sheet,
cash flow, scenarios, a one-page dashboard; delivered in 5 working days
with one revision round and a 30-minute walkthrough.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Build one anonymised showcase model (SaaS or services) and a 10-slide
   "what investors check in your model" PDF as the lead magnet.
2. List founders from public incubator cohorts (QSTP, QBIC, DIC, Qatar
   FinTech Hub) and recent GCC funding announcements.
3. Message 20 founders: free 30-minute review of their current numbers,
   then a fixed quote (USD 900 starter, USD 1,800 full raise model).
4. Scoping call: business model, stage, what the model is for (QDB loan,
   angel round, incubator demo day).
5. Build: agent generates the workbook with live formulas and checks
   (balance sheet balances, cash never negative without a flag); owner
   reviews every assumption and the outputs.
6. Walkthrough; hand over with an assumptions log; offer a pre-round
   update at USD 300.
7. Upwork catalogue project in parallel at USD 900.

## Prices observed
Upwork guidance: cash-flow projection USD 500-1,500; three-statement startup
model USD 1,500-3,500; fundraising/valuation USD 3,000-6,000; median hourly
USD 35; offshore USD 40-80/h; template vendors at USD 109.

## Tools
Excel (owner), Python openpyxl for generation, a checklist of model checks.

## Time per week
Owner 4-6 h at 1-2 models a month.

## Ramp
First dollar 2-4 weeks by direct asks. At 6 months: USD 500-3,000 a month.
Median unknown.

## Risks
Incubators bundling the service; wrong models damage reputation (owner
review mandatory); demand follows cohort and funding calendars.
