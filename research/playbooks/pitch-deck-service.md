# Playbook: investor pitch deck and one-pager service

Checked 2026-11-02. Sources in the brief 20261102-gcc-startup-pitch-deck-service.

## What is sold
A 12-15 slide investor deck (editable PowerPoint plus PDF) and a one-page
teaser, English or bilingual: USD 490 design-only, USD 890 write-and-
design with two revision rounds, one-pager USD 120.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Build three sample decks and a before/after page; Gumroad packages.
2. List founders from public GCC accelerator cohorts and startup
   directories; company addresses only; share the list with the
   financial-model service so no founder is emailed twice in a month.
3. Email 20 then 30 with one slide redesigned from their public material
   and the fixed price; opt-out line.
4. Collect content by a short email questionnaire; draft narrative, lay
   out, deliver in 3-5 days; two revision rounds; delete material after
   30 days.
5. Offer the financial-model bundle and the one-pager; ask for a referral
   inside the cohort.

## Prices observed
Fiverr: typical USD 200-250 (pitch decks), USD 350-400 (funding pitch
presentations); top sellers from USD 160-245 with 1,000+ reviews;
consultants USD 834-1,530. Freelance designers USD 500-3,000 per deck;
top Upwork freelancers USD 200K-600K lifetime at USD 79-100/h.

## Tools
Studio slide tooling (pptx), open-source fonts and icons, the studio
email sender, Gumroad.

## Time per week
Owner 0 h.

## Ramp
First dollar 2-4 weeks. At 6 months: USD 500-2,000 a month. Median unknown.

## Risks
AI deck generators; price-sensitive founders; subjective design
revisions; overlap with the financial-model prospect list.
