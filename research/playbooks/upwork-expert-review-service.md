# Playbook: fixed-fee expert review sold by outreach and on Upwork

Observed method: specialists sell a bounded review (audit, readiness check,
second opinion) at a fixed fee between gig prices (Fiverr schedule builds from
USD 5-10) and expert rates (USD 175-400+/h, JurisPro). Upwork profiles in the
niche list USD 35-65/h and tens of thousands earned; Upwork's freelancer fee
is 0-15% per contract; Fiverr buyers fell 21.9% while spend per buyer rose.

## Steps
1. Define the review as a fixed deliverable with a 2-5 day turnaround and a one-page output.
2. Prepare an NDA template and a secure upload instruction.
3. Build a list of 50 named buyers with a live reason to buy (recent award, re-award or delay news).
4. Send personalised messages offering a free 15-minute look at one issue; convert to the fixed-fee review.
5. Create an Upwork profile and fixed-price project as a second channel.
6. Deliver with the owner's tooling; the owner signs every judgement.

## Tools
The owner's P6 and analysis tooling, Word, PayPal invoicing (withdrawn via QNB), Upwork.

## Time and ramp
- Time to first dollar: 2-6 weeks.
- Typical monthly range at 6 months: USD 500-3,000 depending on owner hours; no median exists.

## Kill signals
Zero signal units from 50 messages; owner hours per review above budget; a client asks for legal opinions the studio cannot give.
