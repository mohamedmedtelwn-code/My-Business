# Playbook: subscription app on the Zid and Salla app markets

Checked 2026-11-05. Sources in the brief 20261105-zid-salla-order-profit-app.

## What is sold
A single-feature subscription app for Saudi and GCC merchants (first
case: profit per order after fees, shipping, VAT and discounts) at
SAR 49 a month per store with a 14-day trial, listed on both markets.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Presale by email first: a free profit statement from an order export
   to 20 then 30 merchants; count "start my trial" replies and payments.
2. Owner opens the Zid and Salla partner accounts and signs the
   agreements (tickets); agents build in development stores.
3. Submit for review on both markets; fix review findings; publish with
   bilingual listing copy and screenshots.
4. Convert the reporting-service clients to the app; every delivery
   carries the link; ask each paying merchant for a review.
5. Reconcile platform payouts in the ledger monthly.

## Prices observed
Zid: 13,500+ stores, 71% use a paid app, 30% median partner revenue
growth; subscription apps on a 20% default rate in the agreement;
non-Saudi partners invoice in USD. Salla: 60,000+ merchants, 500+ app
developers, earnings example SAR 4,130 a month for 100 subscriptions at
SAR 59. Shopify comparison: median listed app under USD 1,000 a month.

## Tools
Zid and Salla merchant APIs and partner portals, a small backend on a
free tier, the studio email sender.

## Time per week
Owner 0 h after the two partner accounts exist.

## Ramp
First dollar 2-3 months (build, review, trial). At 6 months: USD 0-650 a
month; median unknown.

## Risks
Payout transfer method to Qatar unverified; review and agreement delays;
discovery needs reviews; platform-native profit reports.
