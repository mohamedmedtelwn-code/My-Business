# Playbook: financial-model templates sold on Gumroad

Checked 2026-10-29. Sources in the brief 20261029-gcc-financial-model-templates.

## What is sold
A three-statement startup model template (Excel and Sheets) localised for
a region (currency, tax, labour rules, funding-programme outputs), with a
free calculator as the lead magnet and a paid bundle (model plus cap table
and runway tool).

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Start from a working model (the studio's service workbook); add the
   regional inputs and a how-to tab; test with three sample businesses.
2. Free lead magnet: a one-page runway calculator on a landing page with
   email sign-up.
3. Gumroad product at USD 49, bundle at USD 99; licence per team; "not
   financial advice" note; screenshots and a 60-second walkthrough video.
4. Email founders from public cohort pages and funding announcements; the
   template is the downsell for anyone who declines the service.
5. Collect 10 reviews by giving the first buyers a free bundle upgrade.
6. Update quarterly (tax and labour rule changes); email buyers the update.

## Numbers observed
Gumroad: Standard Financial Model USD 149, 53 reviews, USD 7,897+ revenue;
Venture Capital Model USD 349, 19 reviews, USD 6,631+; generic models
USD 29-49 with few reviews; Eloquens USD 104-336; Gumroad median creator
USD 72 a month.

## Tools
Excel, Google Sheets, Gumroad, the studio email sender, landing page.

## Time per week
Owner 0 h after the Gumroad account exists.

## Ramp
First dollar 2-4 weeks. At 6 months: USD 72-600 a month; top listing in the
niche about USD 8,000 lifetime.

## Risks
Free incubator templates, formula errors, low discoverability without the
email list.
