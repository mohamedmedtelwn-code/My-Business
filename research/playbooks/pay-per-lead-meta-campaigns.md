# Playbook: pay-per-lead Meta campaigns for local service businesses

Checked 2026-10-26. Sources in the brief 20261026-qatar-home-services-lead-gen.

## What is sold
Set-up and management of Facebook and Instagram lead campaigns (WhatsApp
click-to-message or instant forms) for one local service business per
niche and city, billed per qualified lead or as a small monthly fee, with
ad spend paid by the client in the client's own ad account.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Pick 3 niches with urgent demand and repeat purchase (cleaning, AC
   maintenance, pest control, moving, tutoring).
2. Define "qualified lead" in writing: a WhatsApp conversation or form with
   name, area and the service needed, within the client's service area.
3. Outreach to 20 licensed firms with under 500 followers: two-week pilot,
   client funds USD 100-150 of ads, pays USD 10-25 per qualified lead.
4. Build: bilingual creatives (3 variants), audience by Doha districts,
   WhatsApp click-to-message objective, lead sheet shared with the client.
5. Run daily checks; kill creatives above target cost per lead; qualify
   leads in the sheet within an hour.
6. Weekly report; invoice leads monthly; offer exclusivity per niche.
7. After the pilot, flat USD 150-300 a month plus per-lead bonus.

## Numbers observed
569 message leads for USD 158 spend (USD 0.28 per conversation) in a Meta
case study for a local service; Qatar/Kuwait CPM USD 7.5-13; Qatar home
services Google CPA modelled QAR 206; local lead values USD 50-300 (US);
25 SEO lead-gen sites earning USD 500/month after 6 months (excluded model:
SEO-only).

## Tools
Meta Ads Manager (client account), WhatsApp Business, Google Sheets, Canva
for creatives.

## Time per week
Owner 2-3 h; agent drafts creatives, reports and the qualification sheet.

## Ramp
First dollar 3-5 weeks. At 6 months: USD 300-1,500 a month with 2-5
clients. Median unknown.

## Risks
Lead-quality disputes, ad-account restrictions, clients taking it in-house,
serving unlicensed businesses.
