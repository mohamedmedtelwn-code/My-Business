# Playbook: spreadsheet automation and dashboards for small businesses

Observed method: freelancers automate a small firm's spreadsheet routines (merges,
clean-ups, macros, dashboards) at USD 500-1,500 per project on Upwork, with
Excel VBA hourly rates at a median of USD 25 (USD 18-40) and dashboard projects
at USD 500-2,500; Fiverr sells generic spreadsheet work from USD 5. Sources:
pipeline brief 20261025-sme-excel-automation-service.

## Steps
1. Pick one recurring pain (month-end consolidation, sales report, stock sheet) and productise it as a fixed-price outcome.
2. Build two sample automations on synthetic data for the portfolio.
3. Send 50 personalised messages to owners and operations staff offering a free 15-minute look at one spreadsheet.
4. Scope in writing (inputs, outputs, one revision), deliver with a how-to page, offer a monthly refresh retainer.
5. List on Upwork and Fiverr with the outcome in the title; raise the price after five reviews.

## Tools
Excel, Google Sheets and Apps Script, Power Query, PayPal invoicing via QNB.

## Time and ramp
- Time to first dollar: 2-6 weeks by outreach.
- Typical monthly range at 6 months: USD 300-1,500 at 1-5 projects a month.

## Kill signals
Zero signal units from 50 messages; average project under USD 150; more than 4 owner hours per project.
