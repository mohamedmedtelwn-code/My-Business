# Playbook: bilingual website privacy-and-consent pack (Saudi PDPL, UAE PDPL)

Checked 2026-11-12. Sources in the brief 20261112-gcc-website-privacy-compliance-pack.

## What is sold
Per website: a before-consent tag scan, a privacy notice and a cookie
notice in Arabic and English written from the scan on SDAIA's guideline
structure, banner wording in both languages, and a developer sheet for
consent-gated tag loading; USD 190, 3 days. Optional USD 19 a month for a
monthly re-scan and notice updates. Not legal advice.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Build the scanner: a headless browser visit that records third-party
   requests before any click, looks for a privacy-policy link and a
   consent banner, and lists the forms on the page.
2. Prospect Saudi and UAE firms of 5-50 staff from public directories and
   the studio's audit lists; scan each site; keep only those with a fault.
3. Email 20 then 30 owners or marketing managers (company addresses)
   naming the tags that load before consent and the missing notice, with
   the fixed price; opt-out line.
4. On reply: confirm the site's tools from the scan plus the client's own
   list, draft both notices and the banner text, deliver with the developer
   sheet and the scan report; invoice.
5. Monthly: re-scan, send a one-line "nothing changed" or an update note;
   invoice the retainer.
6. Free "scan your site" page on the studio's free hosting; Gumroad pack.
