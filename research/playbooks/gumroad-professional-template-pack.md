# Playbook: professional template pack sold on Gumroad

Observed method: specialists package documents they already use (letters,
trackers, registers, worked examples) and sell them as a one-off download to
peers who lack an in-house department. Generic packs sell at USD 6-15 (Etsy,
Digistore24); specialised training in the same field sells at USD 645 (Claims
Class). Sources: research/STUDIO-RESEARCH.md section 5.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Pick a document set buyers search for and cannot get free in their jurisdiction or language.
2. Draft the pack from public standards (here FIDIC clause structure), add one worked example.
3. Open a Gumroad account (country Qatar, bank payout in QAR), list at USD 29-79 with a clear "not legal advice" line.
4. Build a list of 50 named buyers (public LinkedIn profiles) and send personalised messages with a free one-page sample.
5. Post the free sample in the community groups the buyers read; answer questions publicly.
6. After 10 sales, ask buyers for one line of feedback and add the top request to v2.

## Tools
Word or LibreOffice, Excel, Gumroad (merchant of record, 10% + USD 0.50 plus
card processing on direct sales), LinkedIn.

## Time and ramp
- Build: 10-20 agent hours plus 2-4 owner review hours.
- Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks with direct outreach (no distribution data for this niche).
- Typical monthly range at 6 months: USD 100-600 for a niche pack (anchors only; no median exists). Treat anything above as unproven.

## Kill signals
Zero signal units from 50 messages; a free equivalent appears from a consultancy; refund rate above 10%.
