# Playbook: Google Business Profile set-up and review-reply retainer

Checked 2026-11-05. Sources in the brief 20261105-gbp-review-management-service.

## What is sold
One-time bilingual profile optimisation at USD 190 per location and a
USD 120 monthly retainer: every review answered within 24 hours in the
customer's language, four posts a month, edit monitoring, a one-page
report. No review generation beyond a compliant request kit.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Build the 10-point audit (fields, categories, photos, Q&A, reply rate,
   response time, bilingual coverage) from public listing data.
2. Prospect clinics, restaurants, salons, gyms and car services with
   unanswered reviews or missing fields; business addresses only.
3. Email 20 then 30 with the audit of their own listing and the fixed
   set-up price; opt-out line.
4. Client grants Manager access; optimise in 3 days; approve the reply
   tone once; start the retainer.
5. Daily reply run, weekly post, monthly report; landing page with a
   self-serve score and a Gumroad subscription.
