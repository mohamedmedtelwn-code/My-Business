# Playbook: done-for-you Excel and Power BI dashboards for one industry

Observed method: freelancers build dashboards from client spreadsheets at USD
500-2,500 per project (Upwork), USD 25-55/h for Power BI consultants, from USD
195 on Fiverr Pro; 8,100+ generic dashboard gigs compete on Fiverr. The wedge
is one industry's vocabulary and data sources.

## Steps
1. Build one reusable template (here S-curve, earned value, manpower, look-ahead from P6 exports).
2. Produce a portfolio sample from public or synthetic data.
3. Offer a free sample chart from the prospect's own data in 50 personalised messages.
4. Deliver a fixed-scope dashboard with one revision and a how-to page; offer a monthly refresh retainer.
5. List on Upwork and Fiverr with the industry in the title.

## Tools
Excel, Power BI Desktop (free), the owner's P6 export knowledge, PayPal invoicing via QNB.

## Time and ramp
- Time to first dollar: 2-6 weeks by outreach.
- Typical monthly range at 6 months: USD 350-2,000 at 1-5 builds a month.

## Kill signals
Zero signal units from 50 messages; price pressure forces builds under USD 150; data mapping exceeds 4 owner hours per client.
