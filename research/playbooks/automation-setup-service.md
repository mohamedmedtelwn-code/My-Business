# Playbook: fixed-fee workflow automation builds

Checked 2026-11-02. Sources in the brief 20261102-gcc-automation-setup-service.

## What is sold
One automation per order, built in the client's own Make or Zapier
account or as a scheduled Python job: USD 390 single workflow, USD 990
multi-step, USD 150 a month "keep it running" retainer with a stated fix
window. Runbook delivered with every build.

## Steps a small operator follows
1. Write ten fixed-price "recipes" (form to CRM, order to sheet, invoice
   reminders, report emails, WhatsApp order confirmations) with a
   landing page and Gumroad checkout.
2. Prospect SMEs whose public stack shows a manual gap; email 20 then 30
   operations or admin managers proposing one named automation.
3. Scope by email (data volume, edge cases, tools), quote fixed.
4. Client invites the studio email into their Make/Zapier or issues an
   API key (stored as a session secret); build, test, document, hand over.
5. Offer the retainer; propose the next recipe after 30 days.

## Prices observed
Upwork: hourly USD 40-90; single workflow USD 300-800; multi-step
USD 800-3,000. 54-operator rate card: SMB retainers USD 650-1,200;
"fixes and setups, 48-hour turnaround" USD 400-1,200 per job. Vendors:
fixed projects USD 300-3,000, retainers USD 500-5,000.

## Tools
Make free tier for demos, client subscriptions for production, Python
scheduled jobs in the runner, the studio email sender, Gumroad.

## Time per week
Owner 0 h.

## Ramp
First dollar 3-5 weeks. At 6 months: USD 400-2,500 a month. Median
unknown; the survey median to USD 10k/month is 7 months for full-time
operators, not a studio side line.

## Risks
Client access refusals; support expectations; platform price changes;
marketplace beginners at USD 20-40/h.
