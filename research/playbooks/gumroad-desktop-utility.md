# Playbook: small desktop utility sold on Gumroad

Observed method: engineers sell single-purpose Excel or Python utilities to
peers at USD 15-90 (Softedemy), while a SaaS newcomer charges USD 29-79 per
report (ScheduleLens) and viewer tools charge USD 344-990 a year
(ScheduleReader, Steelray). Sources: research/STUDIO-RESEARCH.md section 5.

## Steps
1. Choose a conversion or check that professionals do by hand and that enterprise tools price out of reach.
2. Build it with a permissively licensed parser (PyP6Xer is LGPL; xerparser and xer-reader are GPL and restrict closed-source sale).
3. Make it run locally; privacy (no upload) is part of the offer.
4. List on Gumroad with a short demo video and a free "lite" version (fewer checks) as the lead magnet.
5. Offer a free run on one prospect file in outreach messages; convert on the full report.
6. Keep a changelog; price increases with features.

## Tools
Python 3.11+, PyP6Xer, openpyxl or CSV, Gumroad, a screen recorder.

## Time and ramp
- Build: 20-40 agent hours; owner tests on real files.
- Time to first dollar: 1-3 months.
- Typical monthly range at 6 months: USD 50-400 (anchors only).

## Kill signals
Free equivalents gain traction; conversion under 2% of lite downloads after 100 downloads; support load above 1 h a week.
